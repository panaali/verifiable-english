Not user-visible, but a policy change for the DRI's attention: the `ve` binary crate's lint goes from
`#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` to `#![deny(unsafe_code)]`, with one `#![allow(unsafe_code)]` module
(`rust/ve/src/memory.rs`, a `GlobalAlloc` that forwards to `System`). `ve-kernel`, `ve-profile` and
`ve-core` keep `forbid`. A counting allocator needs this. The other option, a checked size budget at each
allocation site, cannot bound memory globally.
