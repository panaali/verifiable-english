# AR-8 deltas

AR-8 changes only tooling: no file under `ve/` or `lean/` changes, so the checker's answers, wire texts and
Lean statements are unchanged (plan 4.1 items 2 and 3 do not apply; `lake build` and `lean/audit.py` still pass
in the gate). The one Rust source edit rewords a doc comment on the same two lines: the release binary's
SHA-256 is unchanged and still pinned in `lean/checkers.lock` (`python3 tools/rust_build.py --check`).

The deltas below are in what `tools/check_rust_deps.py` and the pre-commit hook accept and print.

| # | Delta | Before | After | Signature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | The intra-crate module-group check is deleted (plan 4.3 AR-8 "Change" and "Deleted") | Each `src/*.rs` module of `ve-kernel`, `ve-profile` and `ve-core` had to be listed in `MODULE_GROUPS`; outward module paths, `#[path]`, `include!`, macro-built paths and similar forms were rejected | A new module in `ve-kernel` or `ve-profile` needs no entry (its crate is its layer); cargo's crate graph is the boundary. Residual: a `#[path]` or `include!` that compiles another crate's file inside a crate is not seen by `cargo metadata`; a follow-up could read rustc's dep-info if wanted | **Needs DRI signature** (HUMAN item, owner: the DRI; deferred, not closable by a fixer): DRI-RULINGS lists deleting a check as always a DRI decision, and no standing ruling covers it, so the review's F4 stays open until the DRI signs or rules otherwise. The deletion is the plan row's own change. What it gives up: a new `ve-kernel` or `ve-profile` module no longer needs a ledger entry (each crate is one group since the split), and the residual above for `ve-kernel` and `ve-profile`. The facade keeps the old protection and more: its `lib.rs` may hold only re-exports (T7), so no `#[path]`, `include!` or inline module can be written there |
