# kb-9: user-visible delta

Feature item kb-9 (architect seat round 4, 2026-11-13; `docs/spec/kernel-boundary.md` decisions 3 and 4, sections 4.1
and 8.1). One existing kind changes, `soft-selected` on the `soft-norms` mode, and only for a case that sends the new
optional member `facts`: a case without it is answered byte for byte as on main (kb-10a's `#guard`s of
`lean/VE/SoftNorms/SelectedExamples.lean`, on cases without `facts`, are unchanged and pass;
`tests/test_kb9_selected_facts.py::test_a_case_without_facts_is_answered_as_before`). No client sent `facts` on a
`soft-selected` case before this change (ve/ and usecases/ on main). The five frozen keys' `results.json` are
byte-identical (each harness's `--check` passed after the switch: docs/kernel/kb-9/after/times.txt).
