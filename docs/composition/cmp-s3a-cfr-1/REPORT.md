## How the proof goes

`plain` only conjoins the clauses and the relation atom to a norm's guard:

- Lines.lean: `linesOf (plain D)` is `(linesOf D.doc).map (hL D)`, so `checkRights (plain D) = checkRights D.doc`, and
  `correlate (plain D)` is `correlate D.doc` with new line-node guards.
- Hyp.lean: with names distinct, that document is `mapG (γP D) (correlate D.doc)`, and every new guard extends the
  old one (`FExt`) by base conjuncts typed in the node's context (`GHyp`). That uses three facts:
  - clause and relation typing (`entry_typed`);
  - no base predicate is regulated or defined, because the base stratum order exists (`layer_names`; its DAG is a
    subgraph of `soften C`'s);
  - no generated rights predicate is an author predicate (`genDecls_fresh`).
- SoftExt.lean, W1Ext.lean, W1Check.lean: every guard-built formula of soft-norms and W1 carries the extension, so
  typing, the stratum DAG (by `VE.NormDefaults.dependencyOrder_congr`) and `semanticCheck` carry over. `checkW1` and
  `check` pass or fail only on the shared AST budget (`ruleLoop_rl`, NormDefaults' `rulesLoop_ok`/`check_iff`).
- SoftCheck.lean, SoftValid.lean: `validateSoft (mapG γ C)` passes or reports a resource limit (`validateSoft_mapG`):
  - the `checkSoft` prefix is unchanged;
  - the tail throws only `ResourceLimit: …` texts around `validateW1 (soften ·)`;
  - `softSemanticCheck` carries over, with the coherence premises through W1's `subst_wellFormed`.
- RightsSem.lean: `rightsSemanticCheck (plain D)` holds (narrowings unchanged, `condF` extended).
- The identifier check reads the same `decisionNames`.

The restated helpers `checkW1Steps`, `checkSoftK`/`shapeK`, `tailK`/`shapeT`, `restT`/`shapeR` and
`checkRightsK`/`restRights` are tied to the trusted functions by `rfl` (`checkW1_eq`, `checkSoft_eq`,
`checkSoftK_shape`, `tailK_shape`, `restT_shape`, `checkRights_eq`), as `VE.NormDefaults.check_eq` ties `checkSteps`.
They copy the statements of the definitions they restate, and the kernel checks each copy against them.
