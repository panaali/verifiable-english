# speed-abcache: a shared, keyed cache of lean-first A/B base recordings

Infra item speed-abcache (PIPELINE-LIMITS L75; acting DRI dri-improve, 2026-10-18 08Z). Every lean-first and
readings batch records the A/B of `docs/LEAN-FIRST-PLAN.md` 5.1 item 2 on both sides: `tools/lean_first_diff.py
record` on a worktree of its base commit and on its head, then `compare`. This item lets a builder record only its
head: the base side comes from a cache of segments recorded once per base commit.
