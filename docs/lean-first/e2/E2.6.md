## What changed

**In Lean (the owner).**

- *The diagnosis of a sentence without a reading* (`VE/Grammar/Expect.lean`, `VE/EnglishGrammar/Diagnose.lean`, spec
  4.5). A recogniser of the grammar finds the farthest token some sentence reaches; Lean then says what the hand
  parser said there: the words the rules read next (`Expected ', then ' here. Use the English paraphrase as a writing
  example.`), the phrase written where a declared wording may come with its three nearest declared wordings
  (`Unknown English phrase 'is a UK citizen'. …`), or the declared wording the text extends (`Phrase '…' extends
  existing wording …`). Its span contains the hand parser's position. `english.read` (both modes) and `senses.read`
  give the same answer. This closes the six weaker readability messages.
- *The lexer* reads an ASCII apostrophe as a word character, as it reads `’`, so a declared wording such as `a system
  prompt's existence-or-content` lexes and passes V3; before, V3 refused the whole lexicon of the constitution
  pilots.
- *V4*: two modal predicates may share their modal form, their one honest wording; a sentence that uses it is
  ambiguous, as the hand parser refuses it. Every other V4 collision stands.
- *The boundary* reads Python's generated soft frame (the soft route's `E′`, marked `generated: true` by the relay) as
  every route reads a generated frame, without its `soft__` decision predicates.
- *The soft route* decodes a constant's `verbalisation` (DF-19), and *the norm-defaults route* admits `data@0` flags,
  refusing a supplied model that violates one (`InvalidModel(flags)`, DF-17 retired). These two are outside English;
  without them readability items R01 to R16, R29 and R30 had no answer at main.

**In Python (deleted or moved to Lean).** The E2.5 shadow dispatch and `SHADOW_ONLY` entry, `parse_english_argument`'s
Python premises and refusal texts, `parse_english_with_metadata`'s Python re-parse, and the relay's nearest-wording
help (`_nearest_help`, `english_phrases.unknown_phrase`). `_python_reading` remains as the comparator and the
statement-layer reader (`english.statement`, until E3).

`actions_version` is 28.
