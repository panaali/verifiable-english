## 1 / 4 — Semantics and boundary

**Decision:** add a versioned `reports@0` statement elaborator and document admission route over existing FOL. Preserve frozen routes, canonical bytes, identities and certificates. Build on [W2 documents](document.md), [W1 norms](norms.md), [W3 definitions](../../PLAN-EXPAND2.md#w3-definitions-lemma-citation-and-statement-layer-sugar) and the [natural view](../natural-view.md), under the [verified-profile rule](../../VISION.md#converged-rule-elaborate-by-default-extend-only-through-verified-profiles-astra--fable-2026-09-25). W2's nested kinds and bridges already prevent quotation-to-premise promotion; they do not yet supply these compositional semantics.

**Content is inspectable.** A quotation contains a closed, typed formula, norm block, definition package, or another report/assessment. Bind its environment, profile versions, recursive content, references and reading choices. Keep source snapshot/span and utterance context in a separate occurrence identity; repeated occurrences may share content. Literal quotation also binds exact words; paraphrase does not claim word identity. Content equality is structural under declared canonicalisation, never equality of truth values or automatic logical equivalence.

**Independent meaning.** A source model is `(S, J)`: an ordinary admissible structure plus arbitrary attitude relations on holders, explicit contexts and structured contents. Write `J_m(h,c,q)` for holder `h` saying, believing, wanting or intending content `q`; `reports@0` admits exactly these four acts. Speech, belief, desire and intention are distinct relations. Promising, pledging, denying, reaffirming faith and determination require distinct reviewed acts and stay UNFORMALISED pending that review; none is silently renamed “intends” or “believes not.” Attribution tense and reference-time bindings are part of the semantic context `c` in the act key, never occurrence-only metadata: “believed” and “believes” generate distinct predicates, so, over the report premises of a document with an admissible `Expandable` structure, a present key that no top-level unit asserts or denies is free (the reading of `unreported_key_free` under its hypotheses, spec 9.2; spec 4.5's tense bullet is its NON-NORMATIVE reading). Outer and complement tense are preserved separately; only supported temporal readings are admitted; dates do not acquire temporal inference from labels.

`J_says(h,c,q)` can hold while `q` is false. Neither `J_believes(h,c,q)` nor `J_wants(h,c,q)` entails `q`; intention entails neither success nor obligation. Nested `says(article, believes(Anthropic,q))` asserts neither Anthropic's belief nor `q` outside that report. Contradictory quoted contents need not make the reporting document inconsistent. Each of these is a statement about the independent semantics, where the attitude relations are arbitrary; as a statement about a document's own premise set it is a statement about the report premises alone (the author's own premises may entail what the report premises do not) and holds exactly as the spec's theorem statements state it, under their hypotheses and no wider (spec 9.2, 9.3): a key that no top-level unit asserts or denies, a content that is a formula over the old language and is neither valid on its own nor entailed by the forced holder disequalities (below), and a consistent premise set. A nested report or assessment content elaborates to a signed generated atom at the nested node's own holder (or evidence context), and what the report premises entail about it is stated on that holder-applied atom with its polarity, never on the holder-free key: entailed when a top-level unit carries the very node, holder and polarity included; refuted when one carries its opposite; free, over a consistent premise set, when no positive top-level premise carries the atom at a co-denoting holder, whether or not the key is asserted at another holder (spec 9.2: `nested_positive_entailed`, `nested_positive_refuted`, `nested_negated_entailed`, `nested_negated_refuted`, `holder_atom_free`; fixtures B₇, B₉ to B₁₂′, `D_ω`). A document that carries the nested node or its opposite at top level, whose disequalities entail the content, whose author premises entail it, or whose signed premises are inconsistent is outside that fragment (spec 2.3, 4.5, 5.3; boundary fixtures 10.3). The spec's normative semantic content is its definitions, its theorem statements with every hypothesis explicit and its fixtures with expected results; its prose readings (spec 4.5, 4.6) are non-normative and claim nothing beyond the statement they name (spec 0, 13.23).

**Elaboration:** for each finite, acyclic, closed content/context/act key, generate a fresh unary predicate on its holder sort; epistemic judgments similarly use predicates on evidence contexts. Boolean combinations use existing FOL; no truth predicate, dynamic quotation or new proof rule is added. The quote table is checked recursively, not an opaque string dictionary. Restriction/expansion between `(S,J)` and generated FOL interpretations must preserve both proofs and countermodels. Fresh attitude relations have arbitrary interpretations: this is vocabulary extension, **not** a W3 definition or a claim of `Layer.expands` uniqueness over `S` alone. Deterministic subordinate layers retain their existing contracts. **Route instance:** the generated predicates are declared in `E_reports` (the author's environment plus one fresh predicate per key); when no deterministic layer applies, the route layer is `Layer.id` with `E_in = E_out = E_reports`, and the translated report premises enter as document premises `Γ`. `Layer.id`'s `Intended = True` describes the empty layer; it neither replaces the independent report semantics nor establishes translation faithfulness, which are two named obligations: `reports_elaboration_faithful` (truth preservation between `(S, J)` and `E_reports` interpretations, with model correspondence in both directions) and `reports_old_language_conservative` (the vocabulary extension adds no assumption over the old language: every admissible old-language structure has an expansion, and old-language consequence is unchanged). Both compose with certificate acceptance (`route_acceptance`) and finite countermodel decoding. The conservativity theorem is about the vocabulary and is unconditional. Removing the report premises themselves is a different statement: P0 has equality and no distinct-name assumption, so the signed report atoms `R(a)` and `¬R(b)` entail the old-language disequality `a ≠ b`. Every theorem that drops signed report premises (well-formed signed atoms over declared constants, spec 4.4) therefore carries exactly the holder disequalities those premises force (the spec's `neqs`), for old-language premises and conclusions, and a document whose report premises are all positive recovers the plain statement; a document asserting and denying one report of one holder is inconsistent on both sides, visibly, through the existing vacuity line.

**Checking inside:** from explicitly selected payloads `P` and `P implies Q`, certify the scoped conditional consequence `Q`. Display “Q follows from the reported contents.” Do not output “the speaker believes Q,” “the article says Q,” or bare `Q`. Payload selection carries occurrence/context IDs; sharing a speaker does not merge contexts. Check inner consistency and vacuity separately; an inconsistent quotation does not automatically supply outer premises.

Quoted W1 rules get a local conflict/completion check but no active outer norm layer. Quoted W3 definitions use a pinned local environment and never redefine outer vocabulary. Unsupported combinations retain the [existing integration gates](../INTEGRATION-GAPS.md); storing content does not certify its elaboration.

## 2 / 4 — English, modality and readings

These **proposed writable templates** await parser implementation. Names and contexts use reviewed vocabulary; technical keys stay in the inspector.

| One-reading wording | Committed scope |
|---|---|
| “The article says that the service is safe.” | Report of the safety claim; safety remains embedded. |
| “Anthropic believes that Claude is helpful.” | Belief with a typed, inspectable helpfulness claim. |
| “Anthropic believed that Claude was helpful.” | Past belief; outer and complement tense retained separately; no present belief and no helpfulness follow. |
| “Anthropic wants Claude to be helpful.” | Desire; no duty or achieved helpfulness. |
| “The team intends to publish the results.” | Intention; no publication fact or promise. |
| “Anthropic believes that Claude is not human.” | Negation inside belief. |
| “It is not the case that Anthropic believes that Claude is human.” | Negation outside belief. |
| “The article says that Anthropic believes that Claude is helpful.” | Two retained attribution scopes. |
| “According to the stated assessment, the service may be safe.” | Open epistemic assessment (`May`) over a bound evidence context; not evidential possibility. |
| “According to the stated assessment, the service is probably safe.” | Open qualitative likelihood assessment (`Probably`) in that context; not a likelihood fact. |
| “Alice is permitted to access the server.” | Permission through W1; distinct from epistemic “may.” |

**Epistemic first slice:** `May(c,q)` and `Probably(c,q)` are named, compositional **open assessments** over structured content. Each carries W2's complete open-term record: declared criteria, evidence context, version and `decided_by`, which is the explicit value `unresolved_decider` (the constitution pilot's precedent) when the source names no assessor, never an absent field or an invented assessor. Admission checks the assessment's structured content and declared criteria; it does not establish evidential possibility or likelihood. The logic around them is exact; whether the assessment applies remains open. They are not probabilities over translation readings. No invented percentage, default threshold, probability calculus, factivity or modal inference is licensed. The parser binds “according to the stated assessment” to the unit's assessment record (its criteria and assessor), never to a `says` report by a holder named “assessment”. Merely attaching a hedge label does not implement either judgment.

If a committed pilot requires modal inference, record the failed elaboration/budget and propose a separate verified profile. A candidate epistemic semantics is `May(c,φ) iff some alternative admitted by c satisfies φ`, with world-relative vocabulary and explicit domain/reference rules. Prove its standard translation and executable model validity before admission; do not assume actual-world accessibility or psychological belief closure. Numerical “probably” requires a source-justified probability model and threshold; otherwise it remains open. This plan does not ship that profile.

**Scope admission:** the all-derivations gate checks the whole sentence and every complement; ambiguity or budget exhaustion rejects. Negation, coordination and quantifiers retain scope inside formula contents. Over report and assessment statements this slice admits negation only, recursively and with its own one-reading wording (“The article says that it is not the case that Anthropic believes that S”); coordination and quantification over report statements have no wording and are deferred until a wording row with one reading exists. No pronoun crosses a quote boundary implicitly. Complements admitted: finite “that”-clauses with closed content, and explicitly specified infinitival templates elaborated to closed content whose embedded subject is resolved per template (“wants Claude to be helpful” binds Claude; “intends to publish the results” binds the holder). Noun-phrase objects (“faith in fundamental human rights”, UDHR `u008a`) have no propositional complement and are out of scope. The first slice requires closed complements with resolved names; de re or cross-boundary bindings stay unresolved until their semantics exists. Desire/duty, promise/prediction and “probably not”/“not probably” are never synonyms.

**Zero-training workflow:** show plain-English alternatives and distinguishing situations; authors choose and approve a meaning-bound sentence. Typed and rendered text must round-trip through the same grammar, including its kind and nested content. An exact round trip preserves the sentence **together with** its bound environment (W2's environment-bound `meaning_id`, extended here to the rendered sentence), contexts, assessment criteria, profile versions and reading choices; bare sentence text never recovers these through mutable defaults. Technical identifiers stay in the inspector, and the manifest that fixes the meaning travels with the sentence. Extend W2-aware presentation and W8 unit actions; formula-only `ve.prose@1` is not a whole-document export. Explicit-form fallback is a visible repair, not a passed English gate.

Translation preserves all admitted readings in extensible versioned sets. Narrowings are separate attributed units. Correlated choices share a key; citations inherit compatible choices, and updates make dependents stale. Use W2's assertion shortcut only with proved shared backgrounds; otherwise check complete choices up to 64, then UNKNOWN. Unelaborated alternatives remain unresolved.

## 3 / 4 — Bridges, assurance and implementation gates

**Bridges are explicit premises.** “For this argument, the reviewer accepts the article's safety claim as true” introduces the exact inner formula as an assumption, with reviewer, occurrence, content identity, environment, choices and reason. For nested reports, name the exact target: accepting the article's complement yields a belief claim; accepting that a report occurred does not. The belief's payload needs its own bridge. An independent proof of the payload can instead be cited with all its dependencies. Testimony, prestige, a URL and a certificate of faithful quotation never discharge truth acceptance.

Norm adoption and definition import are separate typed operations: state the destination context and regenerate validated W1 layers or W3 environments. A formula bridge cannot activate rules or install definitions. An unsupported import stays unsupported. New labels preserve the legacy `bridge:accepts` meaning; never retrofit a different meaning into old records (W2 unit ids may contain dots, so a nested target needs a separate label with explicit unit and path fields rather than a dotted suffix on the legacy one). All used bridges, assumptions, open decisions and narrowings propagate transitively through citations, scoped checks and final conclusions.

| Status | What the report establishes |
|---|---|
| CHECKED | Accepted scope/types/identities and admitted content; any claimed consequence has replayed evidence. Assumptions are identified, not proved true. |
| LABELLED | Source/act/support metadata or retained prose only; no substantive coverage from an empty complement. |
| OPEN | An admitted compositional meaning depends on specified open judgments. Open-term inventories alone do not qualify. |
| READINGS / UNFORMALISED | Elaborated alternatives with witnesses / residual missing meanings. Unelaborated reading lists earn no READINGS coverage. |

**Lean obligations, all pending:** independently define `Intended`, typing and scope; prove elaboration faithfulness at every universe and model correspondence in both directions, including finite witness decoding (`reports_elaboration_faithful`). Prove old-language conservativity without new assumptions (`reports_old_language_conservative`); do not confuse arbitrary attitude interpretations with unique definitional expansion. Prove scoped consequence soundness, bridge/adoption admission and graph composition with exact transitive leaves. Supply executable model validity, countermodel soundness and `CertificateSound` without an assumed soundness oracle. Prove parser/renderer correspondence and one-reading claims for the admitted surface; tests alone do not discharge them. Audit under the existing axiom allowlist and bind exact statements, route/build, profiles, environments, quote tables and choices. ## Summary

NON-NORMATIVE, as every semantic sentence of this plan (page 1). Add structured reported content through a versioned statement elaborator over existing FOL. Preserve speakers, contexts, force, nested content and reading choices. Check embedded formulas, rules and definitions locally: a scoped check is a report line and not a premise (`scoped_not_premise`), adoption and import are typed operations whose provenance field adds no semantics (spec 5.4), and a bridge is the explicit, identity-bound premise by which a reviewer assumes a content for an argument (spec 5.2, `admit_report_sound`). What a document's report premises entail, refute or leave free about a content is stated only by the spec's definitions, theorem statements and fixtures (spec 0, 9.2, 9.3): a formula content over the old language by `reports_no_leak`, a nested content by the five nested-content theorems on its holder-applied atom, under which a nested content that a top-level unit carries is entailed with no bridge (B₇). Saying, belief, desire and intention are the four admitted acts, distinct relations keyed by content and by a context that carries tense; qualitative epistemic assessments stay open, with an explicit unresolved assessor where the source names none and no invented probabilities. Stronger modal inference requires a separately justified verified profile. English must be writable, uniquely parsed and faithfully rendered together with its bound environment, contexts, criteria, versions and reading choices. Lean obligations cover semantics, elaboration, model correspondence, admission and provenance. Pilot accounting separates substantive meaning from labels and inventories, preserves translation alternatives and counts bytes once. Constitution and UDHR tags bound opportunities, not gains; news and grant gains remain unmeasured until admitted translations and fidelity review exist.
