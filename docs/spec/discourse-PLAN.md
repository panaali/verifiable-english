# DISCOURSE and CROSS-REFERENCE: plan

**Proposed, 2026-09-28; baseline `df874bd954aa`. Revised 2026-09-28 with the converged Astra + Fable edits (`manage-agents/debates/design-discourse/`, round 2, edits J1 to J8); revised again 2026-09-28 to mirror the converged statement review of the item-1 spec (`manage-agents/debates/discourse-spec/`, rounds 1 to 3, verdict FIX, applied as [discourse.md](discourse.md) draft 2): typed competitors with the type check as the only filter, explicit target representations, the three adapters to the frozen interfaces, import integrity separated from admission outcome, and the natural-view difference.** Four-page design body below; no implementation or coverage gain claimed. Follows [VISION](../../VISION.md) (self-reference boundary at commit `da8d8e2396cb`), [North Star][north], [W1](norms.md), [W2](document.md), [W3][w3], the [soft route](soft-norms.md) and the [natural view](../natural-view.md).

**Summary (104 words).** Resolve references into explicit, typed targets before checking; preserve the existing logic and its identities. Named antecedents, qualified descriptions, definitions and document anchors receive versioned bindings with source provenance. Each accepted VE sentence has one closed reading; unresolved English produces an IDE question or retained translation readings. Existential witnesses never silently become global names, and links never confer truth or authority. Descriptions expose existence, uniqueness and contextual restrictions. Lean must verify resolution, elaboration, compatible choices and model correspondence. Start with names and navigation, then descriptions and correlated readings, followed by W1/W3 integration. Measure faithful source-byte gains separately from reference resolution, labels and unresolved judgments.

## Page 1 — Meaning and scope

**Contract.** Propose `ve.discourse@1`, a versioned presentation and binding manifest extending W2 through a **new document-format version, `ve-document@2`**, never extra silently accepted `ve-document@1` fields. It elaborates into existing closed formulas, W1 blocks and W3 declarations/citations. No new logical operator, checker rule or leaf label. Freeze old routes, canonical bytes, meanings and certificates; retain `ve.prose@1` behavior. The profile is outside the frozen profile registry, so a wrapper validates it and strips it from the profile list and the environment JSON before every frozen call (as the norms replay strips `norms@0`); environment, meaning and definition identities are computed under that stripped pair, and the wrapper report binds the original profile declaration, the manifest and the `@2` document identity (spec 3.5). New semantics require the verified-profile process in VISION, including a committed failed elaboration case, conservativity, executable model validity, certificate/countermodel soundness and composition proofs.

**Explicit context.** A manifest records document/source identity; ordered section/clause anchors, each listing the declarations it exports; mention IDs and UTF-8 spans, disjoint or properly nested within a unit; target type, scope and kind; environment/definition versions; reading choices; and resolution provenance. Entity targets are declared constants or local binders, each with a sort; plural lists and ordered pairs list their members explicitly; unit targets list their units, optionally under a registered content name; other targets are predicates, rule IDs or certified claims. These types cannot coerce into one another. Names, content names and section numbers are display labels, not identities. Navigation and location bindings produce no formula and never occupy a slot. The checker regenerates bindings from source plus manifest and rejects stale, missing, colliding, ill-typed or inaccessible targets. A reading may bind an ordered list of source spans; each span has exactly one status and is counted once. A fused reading contributes one assertion, regenerated per group and reading, never per fragment; its owning reading set lives on its first span; its spans keep their historical boundaries and receive status through that set, and no fragment acquires an independent assertion or a second reading set. Reading sets may declare compatibility constraints; joint enumeration uses only these.

Let `Accepted(D,s,ρ,A)` independently specify the parses and reference assignments of sentence `s` in document `D` (environment, definition versions and manifest), given the recorded reading choices `ρ`. Acceptance fixes one recorded, typed derivation and assignment and requires every competing derivation and accessible assignment whose elaboration is closed and well-typed to equal it, modulo declared aliases, yielding closed typed `A`; the type check is the only filter on competitors, and recorded targets and reading choices never prune candidates. After uniqueness, the unit's recorded mentions must be exactly the accepted derivation's slots. Selecting one parser result is insufficient. The all-derivations gate includes antecedent and scope alternatives. Resource exhaustion is an error/UNKNOWN, never uniqueness. Logical judgment uses `A`, not mutable conversation history; standalone export repeats its targets.

**Accessibility.** Registered names are reusable throughout their declared document scope; there is no nearest-mention, gender, salience or paragraph-reset heuristic. Accessibility's inputs are explicit: registrations, anchors, declarations, the mention's unit and form, the binders of its own sentence, and the binders, coordinations and bound properties of the earlier sentences of its innermost anchor (the competing-quantifier, ordered-pair and `such` inputs); sorts play no part in accessibility and act only through the type check. A local binder stays inside its sentence/rule. Quantification, negation, disjunction, conditionals, quotation and attitudes cannot export witnesses. A quoted name can be identified without admitting quoted facts. Forward navigation is allowed after indexing; proof and definition dependencies must remain acyclic. Section scope must be explicit, not inferred from typography. A manifest supersedes the natural view's untyped, document-wide `it` rule by sort-typing it; the two agree exactly when every earlier-mentioned constant has one sort, and the spec records the intended difference otherwise (fixture α′).

“A service is deployed. It is reviewed” does not license a fresh constant or two unrelated existentials. The same-witness reading rewrites to “Some service is deployed and reviewed,” meaning `∃x (deployed(x) ∧ reviewed(x))`, with both source spans retained. Other readings remain alternatives. A universal followed by “it” likewise needs an explicit scoped rewrite. Sentence fusion changes reviewed presentation, never historical source boundaries.

**Definites.** Separate declared names (“the API”) from descriptions. For an explicit restriction `D` over a sort `σ`, define `U(D) := ∃x:σ (D(x) ∧ ∀y:σ (D(y) → y=x))`. An assertive unique-description reading of “The owner is ready” is `∃x:σ (D(x) ∧ ∀y:σ (D(y) → y=x) ∧ ready(x))`. A presuppositional reading records `U(D)` separately and checks `∀x:σ (D(x) → ready(x))` only under that displayed premise. `U(D)` enters the claim graph in one of the two frozen forms only, never as a new leaf label: proved, as a certified unit-less asserted deduction node; or assumed, as an explicit asserted unit the IDE offers (“There is exactly one owner.”) and the author signs, named by the mention; otherwise resolution remains pending (translation cannot add the unit). Never infer existence from “the,” uniqueness from one known instance, or truth from a finite lookup. Contextual restrictions and competing presupposition/accommodation readings remain explicit. No choice functions or witness constants are introduced.

## Page 2 — English and cross-reference

These are **proposed admission rules**, not claims that today's parser accepts all examples. Declared vocabulary still controls predicate meanings. All accepted renderings must be writable and round-trip with their bindings.

| Input or situation | One-reading wording / admission rule |
|---|---|
| “The API is reviewed. It is safe.” | The reviewed presentation of the second sentence is “The API is safe.”; the source `It` is retained as provenance. Bare `it` requires one accessible antecedent and no competing quantified mention. |
| Alice and Bob precede “She is ready.” | Ask “Does ‘she’ mean Alice or Bob?” Offer “Alice is ready” and “Bob is ready,” with a distinguishing situation. Never select by name or gender. |
| “This service is safe.” | Bind to a particular named service; display “The API is safe.” Bare `this` referring to a claim/action needs that target kind and its own supported semantics. |
| “Every such service is safe.” | With an explicitly bound property, write “Every reviewed service is safe.” `such` copies exactly that property, not a guessed similarity relation. |
| “The former is ready.” | Bind to the first member of one explicitly identified ordered pair, e.g. Alice in “Alice and Bob”; render “Alice is ready.” Competing pairs require a question. Pair order asserts no inequality. |
| “Her manager is ready.” | First resolve “her” (a mention nested inside the description); then expose the manager relation and uniqueness: “Alice has exactly one manager. Every manager of Alice is ready.” The first sentence is the explicit unit that carries the presupposition when it is assumed rather than proved. Preserve any presupposition reading separately. |
| “They are ready.” | For an explicitly listed finite set of constants and a distributive reading, write “Alice is ready and Bob is ready.” Collective, generic and unknown-group readings stay unresolved; a recorded collective reading is rejected at decode time. |
| “Every eligible service is reviewed, where eligible has the meaning defined in section 2.” | Rewrite as “Eligible has the meaning defined in section 2. Every eligible service is reviewed.” The first sentence selects the exact W3 declaration exported by the pinned section and attaches it to `eligible`; the second is checked as ordinary English with the same universal assertion. |
| “Article 29”; “clause 2.1” | Bind a qualified document/edition and stable container/unit ID. A whole article is a location, not one inferred proposition. |

**Three distinct operations.** “See Article 29 of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights” is a navigation instruction/label. “Eligible has the meaning defined in section 2” selects a pinned W3 declaration, signature and body, from the declarations the anchor explicitly exports; it neither invents a definition nor changes its environment. “By clause 2.1, the API is safe” requests a W3 checked deduction, with matching environment, every cited non-background transitive leaf listed among the citing claim's own hypotheses, compatible reading choices that extend to a validated compatible joint choice of the citing document, and inherited transitive leaves; all retained assumptions, bridges and reading choices remain visible. A link alone performs neither of the latter operations. Missing imported content stays unresolved; no live URL lookup can change committed meaning.

Qualified references include subsection/item paths when needed. Renumbering preserves target identity but refreshes source/display approval; edited target content invalidates dependent bindings and certificates as appropriate. Duplicate numbers, ambiguous editions and dangling anchors fail. Navigation cycles are harmless; inference cycles fail.

W1 exception/precedence references resolve to stable rule IDs and retain W1's same-predicate and DAG restrictions. “Under Article 29” does not automatically import every clause as a premise, establish legal authority, or choose override semantics. Incorporation and authority require explicit admitted content and remain open otherwise.

## Page 3 — Ambiguity, assurance and implementation gates

**Modes.** Authoring asks a short English question, shows expanded alternatives and concrete distinguishing situations, then records the author's signed choice. The expansion is the reviewed presentation in authoring, approval and export; the source pronoun is kept as provenance only. A hidden binding never rescues ambiguous displayed prose. Translation keeps every admitted reading considered so far; its inventory is extensible, never a claim of exhaustive interpretation. A translator's narrowing is attributed and labelled on every dependent result; it cannot replace the source set. Weights never select a referent. Missing semantics or distinguishing evidence leaves a candidate pending, not fictitiously checked.

**Correlated readings.** A choice assigning “this pattern” controls all later references to that same mention. Record dependencies and enumerate compatible *joint* assignments, rather than taking independent per-sentence products. For same-environment/background assertion readings, a joint assignment denotes the conjunction of its sentences (the empty conjunction is `top`: with no open sets there is exactly one, empty, choice); use a disjunction of these conjunctions only after proving correspondence to the compatibility relation. Reuse W2's per-unit shortcut only for independent choices. Otherwise elaborate each complete choice (maximum 64); unsupported kinds/backgrounds or budget overflow yield UNKNOWN. ALL needs proof for every compatible choice; NONE needs checked countermodels for every choice (one shared model may suffice); SOME needs a choice proof and another choice's countermodel. Retain vacuity and global choice/version labels through citations. ALL, NONE and SOME are defined only over a nonempty validated compatible-joint set. An empty set after a completed enumeration is a manifest validation error (an invalid-compatibility diagnostic), distinct from inconsistent premises inside an existing choice, which keep W2's inconsistency and vacuity report; validation that does not finish within budget is UNKNOWN. W3's citation check and W2's choice check merge reading choices within one set only and see no `requires`/`excludes`; discourse admission therefore additionally requires each lemma's transitive choices, and the merged transitive assumptions of every reported graph result, to extend to a validated compatible joint choice (`all` keeps its version and selects no branch); failure after a completed enumeration is an invalid-compatibility diagnostic naming the citation or the report, and an unfinished enumeration leaves the check UNKNOWN.

| Boundary | What is established |
|---|---|
| CHECKED | Source partition, target identity/accessibility, one admitted elaboration, typing, and certificate/model results under listed premises. Assumption support does not prove truth. |
| LABELLED | Navigation, headings, kind/support/provenance and proposed reference inventories. A linked clause or matched pronoun alone gives no substantive coverage. |
| OPEN | Fully represented claims with named W2 open predicates and residual adjudication; unstated deciders stay unstated. |
| READINGS / UNFORMALISED | Complete admitted alternatives versus missing content/semantics. Unelaborated inventories remain UNFORMALISED; never rename ambiguity as an open predicate. |

**Lean obligations, all pending.** Independently review `Accepted`, accessibility and description semantics before implementation. Prove (1) executable resolution soundness, supported-fragment completeness/termination, the unique accepted derivation and assignment with the type check as the only competitor filter, slot correspondence with the recorded mentions, capture-avoiding substitution and closedness; (2) `Holds(S, elaborate(D,s,ρ)) ↔ Intended(D,s,ρ,S)` for every admissible structure, including description definedness at the binder sort and same-witness rewrites; (3) unchanged environments/models for substitution, preservation of old denotations/consequence under a fresh document sort with fresh constant names, finite evaluation and countermodel correspondence; (4) joint-choice equivalence (empty conjunction `top`, emptiness only after a completed enumeration), version-compatible citation composition with W3's per-set meaning retained, extension of every lemma's and every reported result's choices to a compatible joint choice, and retention of assumptions/kinds/bridges; (5) English parse/render correspondence with `Meaning := Formula` under a lexicon-coverage hypothesis, exact source/manifest/expected-statement binding with identities computed under the stripped profile pair and the wrapper binding the original declaration, and stale-dependency rejection stated over the bound evidence envelope; (6) the adapter contracts: the frozen gate's verdict on the base list unchanged, the presupposition carriers as frozen `asserted` forms with no `discourse:*` label in any report, and validity in a second phase with rejection (a completed non-resource diagnostic) distinct from UNKNOWN and import integrity separated from outcome. Every theorem carries typed hypotheses, never placeholder binders. Bind any actual vocabulary extension through P0 `Layer` obligations; do not present existential witness naming as W3's unique conservative expansion.

Audit proofs under the existing axiom allowlist; no `sorryAx`, added domain axioms or assumed checker soundness. Decoding, hashing and display remain named trust boundaries until refined. An untrusted resolver may propose bindings; a small verified validator authorizes them. Python/Rust/Lean agreement is regression evidence, not these proofs.

**Existing limits matter.** [Integration gaps](../INTEGRATION-GAPS.md) still block Lean mixed W1/W3/data cases, defined-environment document graph admission and a full W2/norm natural-view round trip. Current [natural rules](../natural-view.md) admit only narrowly placed `it` with one previously mentioned constant and no earlier quantifier, untyped and document-wide; plural/possessive forms require repair. The manifest sort-types that rule, so the two agree only when every earlier-mentioned constant has one sort. This plan adds no assurance to those paths. Compositions with the soft route are supported only with its coherence premises and conditional guarantees retained unchanged; every other composition remains explicitly unsupported.
