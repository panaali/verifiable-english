## 1. Decision and evidence

Make **COMMANDS** (`instruction`) and **QUESTIONS** (`question`) first-class typed content, retaining W2's existing kinds. A command directs an addressee to act; a question requests a response satisfying an answer contract. Neither asserts its embedded proposition. Checking a request's meaning is separate from checking an answer or evidence of compliance. Merely recognising an imperative or question mark earns no semantic coverage.

**Evidence inspected.** The [web catalogue](../../../ve-webapp/webapp/vocabulary/catalogue.json) at web commit `ad1bb360` records **265 SENTENCE segments: 120 without gaps, 145 with SyntaxError; 129 LABEL segments; five dynamic gap paths**. These are stored parser classifications, not a fresh audit or source-byte coverage. Catalogue SHA-256: `f6e343808b098da34b2376d28fd4bf10ce55c5f0dd2db6e5ff1a56e061265bfa`. Its [measurement README](../../../ve-webapp/webapp/vocabulary/README.md) explicitly names commands/questions as gaps and requires a runtime policy for dynamic text. Examples include “Open the writing desk.”, “Choose authoring or translation mode.”, “Change example now?” and “Did every sampled district report a drop?” Other failures concern time, quantities, causation, definitions and vocabulary; 145 is not a speech-act opportunity count.

The [current tutorial](../onboarding/ve-tutorial.html), especially its worked arguments and demo instructions, asks whether conclusions follow and tells readers to choose examples. [VE's README](../../README.md) also mixes executable command examples, explanations and instructions. Code samples are quoted content, not automatically active instructions. In [OpenStax, Elementary Algebra 2e, chapter 3 review exercises](https://openstax.org/books/elementary-algebra-2e/pages/3-review-exercises) (accessed 2026-09-28), exercises 339–340 request reflection and explanation; 341 asks for a population count; 342 directs the student to find a count. This small evidence sample motivates distinct response contracts, not a textbook coverage estimate.

**Build on the merged boundary.** Reuse [W1 norms](norms.md) for obligations/prohibitions, [W2 documents](document.md) for spans, kinds, readings and open terms, [W3 definitions](../../lean/VE/Definitions/STATEMENTS.md) for typed aliases/bodies and dependency-preserving citations, and the [natural view](../natural-view.md) for writable/rendered English parity. W2 labels already exist; speech-act elaboration does not. [Integration gaps](../INTEGRATION-GAPS.md) still gate W1/W3/data combinations, non-assertion readings, W8 authoring and full-document natural rendering.

Use a new opt-in `speech-acts@0` document route, with `ve-document@2` content and `ve.prose@2` presentation. Preserve old routes, IDs, canonical bytes, certificates and printers. Start with existing FOL/W1 fragments; reject unsupported combinations. Follow [VISION](../../VISION.md): elaborate by default; any inference needing new logic requires a committed pilot failure, independently specified semantics and a verified profile. No temporal, causal, epistemic or execution logic is smuggled into an action name.

## 2. Semantics and boundaries

**Command.** Store issuer, typed addressee, context/scope, force (`require` or `forbid`), declared act and arguments, guard, and W1 policy/rule reference. The context explicitly binds “you”, definite objects and the applicable task/session through a **writable, reader-visible legend** (section 3): a `speech-acts@0` environment pins exactly one scope per document (task, addressee, definite objects) and renders it as legend sentences, as W1 renders `By default,` (norms.md 3.1, 13.4); a binding may also be authored in the source as a typed declaration unit. A command with an unbound slot is a repair case, never a demand on every reader forever. The issuer's authority is provenance or a named assumption, not a theorem.

Independently interpret a direct command as a document-level demand on that addressee in that scope. Its normative realization uses W1's applicability, selection and completion semantics: positive force contributes `required_act(args)`; negative force contributes `¬permitted_act(args)`, **not** `¬required_act(args)`. Bind the addressee/objects with equality guards in a typed W1 rule whose consequence atom is the declared act's regulated predicate. Regulated declarations, roles, duals and defaults are declared explicitly in the `speech-acts@0` environment exactly as norms.md 3.1 requires, so the legend renders; a prohibition whose act has no declared permission role is rejected with a named diagnostic (`MissingPermissionRole`), never realised as `¬required`. Never silently replace a document's defaults. Unscoped acts use the pinned task context, not invented time arguments. **Scope affects semantics, not only identity.** W1's situation variables are exactly the act's arguments (norms.md 1) and carry no task coordinate, so `@0` requires one pinned semantic scope per W1 policy evaluation: separately scoped documents cannot be combined into shared regulated relations merely because each scope field is valid; a combination of documents with different scopes is rejected (`ScopeMismatch`) unless a separately verified scope-preserving encoding is declared, and “Open the desk” in task A beside “Do not open the desk” in task B is never a CONFLICT. N-ary or temporal requirements beyond the admitted route remain unsupported.

An active authored directive supplies the named norm to that document's policy. A quotation, example or reported directive supplies none. Adopting it requires a separate attributed adoption with a verified norm bridge and retained provenance. Requests, advice and invitations are not automatically hard duties; ambiguous source force stays in a reading set. “Please” may preserve force only after approval. Permission-granting utterances (“You may close it.”) are outside this route's force set `{require, forbid}`; they are W1 rule material or unsupported, never a command. Norm checking can establish requirements/conflicts; it cannot establish execution, authority, ability, deadlines or fulfilment. Reading or checking a command never executes it.

**Question.** Store asker, addressee, context, response mode, domain, presuppositions, and a typed answer contract. **The response mode is derived from the wording, never authored:** plural “Which of A and B are P?” is exhaustive; singular “Which one of A and B is P?” is single-answer with the existence and uniqueness presupposition; a stored mode field that disagrees with the wording is rejected at admission (`ModeMismatch`), so one sentence never has two stored meanings. Every question has a closed **presupposition formula `H(Q)`** in the pinned environment (`⊤` for a polar question; existence and uniqueness for single mode; nothing for a restricted named domain) and, for an ordinary finite question, one closed correctness formula `F(a)` per response token `a`. Answers are governed by both on both paths: `Answers(Q,M) = {a | M ⊨ H(Q) ∧ F(a)}`, and a proof-supported answer proves `H(Q) ∧ F(a)`. A polar question about `P` has `{yes ↦ P, no ↦ ¬P}`. A finite “which” question has one formula per named alternative; an exhaustive answer `S` requires all selected alternatives true and every unselected alternative false. An empty exhaustive answer is legitimate. A deliberately restricted named domain (“of Atlas and Beacon”) carries no completeness presupposition; only an open-domain “which” carries the empirical claim that the listed names exhaust a wider domain, and that claim is an explicit presupposition, never a fact inferred from asking.

Given admissible premises `B`, an answer is supported by a replayed certificate for `B ⇒ H(Q) ∧ F(a)`, or conditionally by a certificate for `B ∧ H(Q) ⇒ F(a)` with `H(Q)` retained as a named assumption in the residual (such a certificate supports the answer conditional on that assumption; it does not establish the presupposition); or it is evaluated only in an explicitly supplied valid model. On the model path `M ⊭ H(Q)` is reported as PRESUPPOSITION-FAILED, separately from a negative answer and never as “no”. Lack of proof is UNKNOWN, not “no”. A countermodel to `B ⇒ P` does not establish `B ⇒ ¬P`. **Consequence questions** instead request a certificate of entailment or a checked countermodel for the pinned `B,P`, reported with the debate protocol's judgments `follows`, `gap` and `unresolved` (docs/spec/debate-protocol.md); `refuted` is not imported, because a countermodel is never read as `B ⇒ ¬P`. Search exhaustion is `unresolved`, a third report outcome, not a truth-valued answer. Every answer retains its assumptions, evidence kind and vacuity status.

“Which reading did you mean?” binds a reading-set version and asks about the addressee's intent. A signed nomination records testimony/author choice, not proof of intent or fidelity; none/multiple/outside-set responses reopen clarification. “Prove that …” requests a certificate for a pinned premise/target pair; it never adds the target as a premise. “Answer question 2.” requests a linked response, without making every question an obligation. Explanations and unrestricted mathematical solutions need declared rubrics or richer profiles; unsupported content stays visible.

## 3. English, admission and honest reports

These are proposed one-reading wordings in the shape of norms.md 2.1: a wording pattern with slots, the one-reading paraphrase and the formal effect. Imperatives reuse the declared W1 act phrase (norms.md 2.4: `{0} click {1}` gives “Click Check.” and `{0} share {1}` gives “Do not share the token.”), so elaboration to a W1 rule with equality guards is mechanical. Every reading is conditional on the legend, which binds `you`, each definite object and the task; the legend is itself writable English. Notation belongs in the inspector, never in reader-facing text.

| Wording pattern | The one reading | Formal effect |
|---|---|---|
| `This document is task <task>.` / `In this task, you are <constant>.` / `In this task, the <noun phrase> is <constant>.` (legend) | The scope of every command and question below is this task; “you” names that addressee; that definite phrase names that object. | scope block: `task`, `addressee`, `definites`; no premise |
| `<Act phrase with slot 0 dropped, later slots filled by a bound name>.` e.g. “Click Check.” | This instruction requires its addressee, in this task, to perform the declared act on the named object. No claim that the act occurred. | W1 rule, sign `+` on the act's obligation predicate, guard `x0 = you ∧ x1 = Check`: `required_click(you, Check)` |
| `Do not <act phrase, same shape>.` e.g. “Do not share the token.” | This instruction forbids its addressee's declared act on the bound object in this task. The act's permission role must be declared; no invented definition of disclosure. | W1 rule, sign `−` on the act's permission predicate: `¬permitted_share(you, token)` |
| “Open the writing desk.” | Require the bound opening act; opening, reading and editing are distinct declared acts. | `required_open(you, desk)` |
| `Is <constant> <predicate wording>?` e.g. “Is Atlas reviewed?” | Request whether the declared property holds of Atlas. | polar: `H = ⊤`; `F(yes) = reviewed(Atlas)`, `F(no) = ¬reviewed(Atlas)` |
| `Which of <A> and <B> are <predicate wording>?` | Request the complete subset of these two named alternatives satisfying that property, including none. | exhaustive over `{A, B}`: `H = ⊤`; answer `S` correct iff `⋀_{a∈S} P(a) ∧ ⋀_{a∉S} ¬P(a)` |
| `Which one of <A> and <B> is <predicate wording>?` | Request the one named alternative satisfying that property; that exactly one does is a presupposition shown in the residual. | single over `{A, B}`: `H = (P(A) ∨ P(B)) ∧ ¬(P(A) ∧ P(B))`; `F(a) = P(a)` |
| “Which reading did you mean?” | Request one intended reading from the displayed, versioned alternatives; intent and the singular presupposition remain explicit residuals. | mode `reading` bound to a reading-set id and version; a signed nomination is testimony |
| “Does it follow from these premises that Tom is an animal?” | Request an entailment certificate or countermodel for the displayed premises and target. | mode `follows` over the pinned `(B, P)`: `follows` / `gap` / `unresolved` |
| “Prove that Tom is an animal using these premises.” | Require submission of an accepted certificate for that exact query; the task may be unsolved or impossible. | command on the declared act `{0} submit a certificate for {1}` with the pinned query as object; compliance evidence is a replayed certificate for exactly `(B, P)` |

All accepted input, including rendered output, passes complete all-derivations checking across assertions, norms, commands and questions. Multiple derivations or exhausted parsing budget reject admission. An action declaration cannot hide an entire sentence behind an opaque predicate and claim fidelity. Use typed slots and W3 aliases only where their meanings are declared; show a paraphrase and distinguishing situation for alternatives.

“Must Tom be a cat?” can mean necessity or duty; “Can you open the desk?” can ask ability, permission or make a request. Preserve each plausible source reading in translation. “Click Check and save.” has coordination/order questions; do not erase “then”, purpose clauses or timing by splitting it. Authoring offers plain-English rewrites and binds approval to meaning and presentation. Translation keeps all admitted alternatives and unresolved candidates; a cited narrowing never overwrites the original set. Treat inferred synonymy likewise.

Report **meaning admitted**, **norm outcome/conflict**, **answer evidence and its scope**, and **compliance evidence** separately. A well-formed unanswered question can have checked semantics; it has no checked answer. A checked proof submission establishes certificate validity, not the student's understanding. Physical completion requires external evidence; “clear explanation” stays open with W2's decider/criteria/version, or unformalised if no adequate contract exists.

Bind the content kind, force, participants, scope, response mode/domain, presuppositions, environment/profile versions, premise/target IDs, reading choices and transitive dependencies into the new identities. Regenerate all elaborations from decoded content. Never admit the body of a question or command as an assertion leaf; answer-to-claim and adoption bridges must state exactly what evidence licenses. Quoted and hedged nesting retains W2 provenance. Reading sets list readings considered so far, never claim exhaustiveness; intent weights cannot affect checks. For non-assertion sets, elaborate complete compatible choices separately (existing limit 64); otherwise UNKNOWN. The assertion-disjunction shortcut is inapplicable.

## 4. Proof gates, delivery and pilot accounting

**Lean obligations (new route; existing checker unchanged).** Review direct command/answer semantics independently of the elaborator. Prove typing/substitution and deterministic elaboration; command faithfulness to W1, including polarity/defaults; question-answer correctness for polar, single and exhaustive responses, with `H(Q)` on both the proof and the model path; proof-task and consequence-response soundness using the production checker. **Exact consequence correspondence:** the consequences of a speech-act document are precisely those licensed by the pinned premises and the regenerated active W1 layers (the author's norm units plus the elaborated active commands, composed by W1's route theorems); no embedded proposition of a command or question becomes an assertion merely through its occurrence in that command or question. Ordinary composition is preserved: from an admitted requirement and an explicit premise `required_open(Alice, desk) → reviewed(Atlas)` the checker may derive `reviewed(Atlas)`. **Dormant-request invariance:** over an unchanged environment and Background, adding, removing or answering a dormant unit (a question, a quoted or example command, an unanswered request) leaves the regenerated premise list and every verdict unchanged; a change to the scope block or to a regulated declaration is a declaration change, handled separately as a new environment identity. Reuse W1's conditional expansion/conflict theorems, not unconditional conservativity for adding duties. Reuse W3's conservative definitions only in admitted combinations.

Require executable admission, regenerated Background validity, model/countermodel correspondence, universe-general certificate soundness without a soundness premise, identity/provenance binding, bridge composition, old-route gates and an end-to-end report theorem. Audit under the existing Lean axiom allowlist. New logic additionally owes conservativity and `CertificateSound`. Declare the surface proof boundary; tests cannot prove English understanding.

**Coverage.** Partition every source UTF-8 byte once, including punctuation, quotations and mixed units; report bytes/units by kind and C/L/O/R/U. C requires substantive admitted meaning, not completion or answer truth; for a command or question, C additionally requires every context slot (addressee, definite object, task) bound and every presupposition formalised and either proved or retained as a named assumption in the residual. L is metadata only and applies only to non-substantive spans; O retains open judgments; R retains fully elaborated unresolved alternatives; U includes any unelaborated reading, unbound slot or unsupported component. Apply U, then R, then O, then C: failing C alone does not make a unit U (fully elaborated alternatives such as necessity versus duty in “Must Tom be a cat?” are R; an open term such as “clear explanation” is O). Environment-declared bindings and defaults are rendered as the legend, not as document units (norms.md 13.4): they sit outside the byte partition and take no status. A binding authored in the source is a typed declaration unit counted C or U like any other unit; a legend sentence that carries a normative default or a factual assertion is substantive and is never L. W2 stores readings under OPEN: supply a disjoint reporting projection and reconcile totals without rewriting old reports. Count open terms, named assumptions, bridge leaves and unresolved sets, retaining per-reading residuals under R. No C gain for guessed deciders or opaque action glosses.

**Expected unlock and risks.** The [North Star portfolio](../../../ve-northstar/NORTH-STAR.md) gives W/D/V weight 3 and T weight 1; speech-act byte opportunities remain unknown. Expect direct gains in web instructions/clarifications and tutorial tasks, then exercise prompts whose underlying mathematics already fits. Reflection, general mathematics, runtime messages, time and causation remain co-blockers. C/U/A/Y may gain explicit directives, not wholesale norm/rights coverage. Measure gains against identical source hashes, separating rewrites and relabelling; do not sum overlapping blockers. Main risks are false normative authority, hidden scope/presuppositions, answer-as-truth badges and training-dependent English. Explicit contexts, separate evidence reports, reading preservation and the human fidelity gate address them; none promises universal coverage.

## Summary

Add typed commands and questions through a versioned document profile while preserving VE's checker and existing meanings. Commands express scoped demands on named addressees, bound by a writable legend within one pinned scope per document, and elaborate to W1 obligations or prohibitions. Questions carry explicit response domains, wording-derived response modes, presuppositions `H(Q)` and answer contracts; consequence questions and proof exercises reuse certificate and countermodel checking under `follows`, `gap` and `unresolved`. Neither construct asserts its embedded content or executes an action. Distinguish admitted meaning, supported answers, presupposition failure, normative consequences and external compliance evidence. Bind context and reading choices into identities; preserve translation alternatives and open judgments. Require Lean faithfulness, exact consequence correspondence, dormant-request invariance, admission and bridge proofs, cross-language conformance, and untrained-reader fidelity gates. The immediate opportunity is web copy and tutorial tasks, followed by bounded textbook exercises. Remeasure source bytes before claiming gains; the current web parser failures are not a semantic unlock estimate.
