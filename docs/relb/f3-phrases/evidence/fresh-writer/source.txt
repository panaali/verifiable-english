F1. Source: The two persons share accommodation as their mutual home in the qualifying week (S1 reg. 1(4)(b), taken as a fact). Here the first person is the applicant and the second is the housemate. This holds.
F2. Source: The first person is a partner of the second (S1 reg. 1(3), taken as a fact, O3). Here the first person is the applicant and the second is the housemate. This holds.
F3. Source: The benefit has been, or falls to be, paid to the person for the qualifying week. Here the person is the applicant and the benefit is the State Pension. This holds.
F4. Source: The person has a first day of ordinary residence in England and Wales. Here the person is the applicant. This holds.
F5. Source: The person's ordinary residence in England and Wales has not ended. Here the person is the housemate. This does not hold.
F6. Source: The person has become ordinarily resident in Scotland or Northern Ireland. Here the person is the housemate. This holds.
F7. Source: The person is detained in custody from the custody start date to the release date. Here the person is the applicant. This holds.
F8. Source: The person's custody is under a sentence imposed by a court. Here the person is the applicant. This holds.
F9. Source: The person's care stay has a last day, the care end date. Here the person is the applicant. This holds.
F10. Source: The person made a claim for the winter fuel payment on the claim date. Here the person is the applicant. This does not hold.
