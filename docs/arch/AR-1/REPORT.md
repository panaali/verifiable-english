## What changed

- `lean/audit.py --write` generates `lean/VE/Audit.lean` and `lean/ResponseShape/Audit.lean` from
  `lean/declarations.txt`: every constant of kind `theorem` with a source range, split by module prefix
  (`ResponseShape` vs the rest), sorted by name, an import per declaring module, header
  "generated by `lean/audit.py --write`; do not edit". It then rewrites `lean/axioms.txt` as before.
  `--check` (the default) fails when either manifest or the record differs from the regenerated text.
- Converged edit: the audit fails when a module's SHA-256 differs from the one `declarations.txt` records
  for it, or when the module sets differ (the two manifests excepted: they are its outputs and are
  compared byte for byte). A manifest regenerated from a stale declaration list cannot pass.
- The theorem-name regex scan is deleted: 18 lines (`evidence/manifests.json`, `theorem_regex_scan_deleted`,
  0 left in HEAD). The forbidden-word scan, the admit-tactic scan and the `#print axioms` allowed set
  (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`) are unchanged.
- The Prop-class instances Lean compiles as theorems are now audited: `VE.Discourse.instLawfulBEqFormula`,
  `VE.Discourse.instLawfulBEqTerm`, `VE.Order.instLawfulBEqValue`.
- `lean/audit.py` splits each manifest's `#print axioms` lines into runs of at most 400 theorems. Each run
  imports only the modules that declare its theorems, and all runs go at once (16 here: 14 VE, 2 ResponseShape).
  `#print axioms` prints each theorem alone, so the runs' outputs in order are the manifests'. The committed
  manifests are still compared byte for byte with the generated text, and `axioms.txt` and every check are
  unchanged. Why: one `lean` over the 5,330 VE theorems takes 600 s CPU, which was 1,365 s wall on this loaded
  host. `test_lean_transitive_axiom_allowlist` (timeout 1,800 s) timed out with one run per manifest at load
  average 140 to 165. With runs of 400 it took 606 s at load 138 to 160 (`evidence/checks.json`,
  `audit_wall_time_seconds`).
- `tools/lean_statements.py` takes statement names from the manifest generated from its own dump and
  records each manifest's generated hash, so `--check` stays byte-identical after `audit.py --write`.
- `tools/regen_generated.py` (eff-merge has merged, `ve-green-eff-merge`): the "lean-audit-manifests" entry
  is now generated (`audit.py --write` / `--check`), not a hand `union`. It owns `axioms.txt` too and replaces
  the old "lean-axioms" entry. It runs after "lean-statements" (which writes `declarations.txt`) and
  before "frozen-inventory" (which hashes `VE/Audit.lean`). Tests are in `tests/test_regen_generated.py`.
- The `example` that pins the approved statement of `coh_rank_valid` moved from the hand-written
  `VE/Audit.lean` to `VE/SoftNorms/Status.lean`, beside the theorem. It is an `example`, so no declaration is
  added or changed.

## First full gate (e61f9d01, failed) and the fixes

The first full gate on e61f9d01 failed on 4 of 14,413 tests (14,316 passed). It ran detached with
`--workers 6`: under a load average of 45 to 95 on 32 cores, the suite alone took 2 h 8 min, longer than one
harness command may run. The 4 failures:

- `test_readings_defaults_rd6b_report.py::test_trust_growth_is_rd6b_entry_and_listed_for_signature` compares
  `docs/trust-baseline.json` per file with the tree. The generated manifests and the moved `example` changed
  the T, P, o and line counts of three files. K, S, D, G, tied and trusted did not change. Fix:
  `trust_map.py --write` (no `--reason`, no history entry).
- `test_gate_integrity.py::test_interrupted_gate_cannot_record_pass` (3 cases) fails in every full gate since
  rd-6b B2 (main 8b9927f2), not only here. None of the other worktrees' gates I found (devtime-1, arch-AR-3,
  arch-AR-4, ts-B1, rd-5) includes 1fc602bd. Inside the gate, HOME is the gate's scratch home, the test fakes
  the toolchain check, and `lake_outputs.lean_executable` fell back to `Path.home()/.elan`: "lean is required
  to check Lake's executables against their traces". Fix in `tools/lake_outputs.py` (no branch in flight
  touches it): fall back to the account's home from the password database, as `tools/gate.py` does, with
  a regression test in `tests/test_lake_outputs.py` (seeded bug S1). I avoided `tests/test_gate_integrity.py`
  (it overlaps infra/arch-AR-3, infra/arch-AR-4 and infra/oom-rootcause) and `tools/gate.py` (it overlaps
  infra/arch-AR-3, infra/arch-AR-4 and infra/ts-B5).

The failed record is kept as `evidence/gate-result-e61f9d01-failed.json`.
