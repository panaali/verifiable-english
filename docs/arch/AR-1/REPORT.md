## What changed

- `lean/audit.py --write` generates `lean/VE/Audit.lean` and `lean/ResponseShape/Audit.lean` from
  `lean/declarations.txt`: every constant of kind `theorem` with a source range, split by module prefix
  (`ResponseShape` vs the rest), sorted by name, an import per declaring module, header
  "generated by `lean/audit.py --write`; do not edit". It then rewrites `lean/axioms.txt` as before.
  `--check` (the default) fails when either manifest or the record differs from the regenerated text.
- Converged edit: the audit fails when a module's SHA-256 differs from the one `declarations.txt` records
  for it, or when the module sets differ (the two manifests excepted: they are its outputs and are
  compared byte for byte). A manifest regenerated from a stale declaration list cannot pass.
- The theorem-name regex scan is deleted: 18 lines (`evidence/manifests.json`, `theorem_regex_scan_deleted`,
  0 left in HEAD). The forbidden-word scan, the admit-tactic scan and the `#print axioms` allowed set
  (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`) are unchanged.
- The Prop-class instances Lean compiles as theorems are now audited: `VE.Discourse.instLawfulBEqFormula`,
  `VE.Discourse.instLawfulBEqTerm`, `VE.Order.instLawfulBEqValue`.
- `tools/lean_statements.py` takes statement names from the manifest generated from its own dump and
  records each manifest's generated hash, so `--check` stays byte-identical after `audit.py --write`.
- `tools/regen_generated.py` (eff-merge has merged, `ve-green-eff-merge`): the "lean-audit-manifests" entry
  is now generated (`audit.py --write` / `--check`), not a hand `union`. It owns `axioms.txt` too and replaces
  the old "lean-axioms" entry. It runs after "lean-statements" (which writes `declarations.txt`) and
  before "frozen-inventory" (which hashes `VE/Audit.lean`). Tests are in `tests/test_regen_generated.py`.
- The `example` that pins the approved statement of `coh_rank_valid` moved from the hand-written
  `VE/Audit.lean` to `VE/SoftNorms/Status.lean`, beside the theorem. It is an `example`, so no declaration is
  added or changed.
