## How the proofs go (lemmas the read-only files could own)

`fill (supU N)` is not `soften` of any document, so S2a's simulation (`faithful_sim`) is applied to the author rules (`author_sim`), and `fill` is added on each side. A filled tree decides as its author rules do, or by `F_p` where the gap says yes and they are silent (`decidesD_fill_lite`). `fill_sim_at` combines the two at any structure that encodes `c`. The filled Lemma K (`pullF_canon_layer`, `pullF_canon`) follows S2a's `pull_canon` through `fill` and uses `fill_sim_at` at each regulated layer.

A choice's document has no `WellFormedU`. `FillLite` lists what `Valid (fill U)` and the filled decisions read. `fillLite_choiceU` derives it from S2a's choice facts (`choice_valid`, `choice_precedence_acyclic`, `choice_over_closed`, `choice_names_nodup`) and from `WellFormedU (supU N)`. `valid_fill_lite` gives `Valid (fill (choiceU N c))`.

Lemmas placed in `SoftSup.lean` that the read-only Readings files could own:
- `unsaid_not_decision` (SupEnv);
- `nameMap_unsaid` (Live, beside `nameMap_frame`);
- `supU_D` and `choiceU_D` (Unspecified);
- `wellFormedSD_parts` and `hyps_sd` (SoftDefaults).

The `*_lite` lemmas restate NormDefaults' `UValid` lemmas over `FillLite`. This is a duplicate proof pattern, filed in FOLLOW-UPS.md under `## sc-10a`.
