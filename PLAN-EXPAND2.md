and an explicit residual: every source span is one unit with a declared kind; every commitment is checked, or rests

and Lean-proved; the English stays readable; `ve assist` and `ve propose` author every construct.

## 2. Design rules (binding for every workstream)

1. **Three trust tiers.** (a) *Checker*: Lean tableau checkers, decoders, identity. Only W4 (and numbers2) change
   it. (b) *Statement layer*: everything that decides what a signed-off sentence means: elaborations, generated
   premises, definitions, unit schema, reading-set rules. Each piece is a Lean-proved elaboration with a
   faithfulness theorem or a reviewed schema; every theorem statement gets a statement review. (c) *Surface and
   tools*: English, `ve assist`, `ve propose`, Rust, search: untrusted, differentially tested.
2. **Elaborate, do not extend the logic** (VISION.md section 6 records the disagreement and the decision). New
   constructs elaborate deterministically into the checked language through one composed route
   (`elaborate = norms ∘ definitions ∘ data`, section 3.4). Each layer carries a Lean faithfulness theorem stated
   relative to the structures of the layer below; the checker is a parameter of the route. The certificate binds
   the elaborated premises to the hash of what the author saw.
3. **Identity freeze.** Existing envelopes (`ve.core@0`, `ve.core@0.1` closure, order) keep every identity,
   canonical byte, certificate, wire hash and theorem statement. New constructs live in profiles under
   `ve.core@0.2` with a sorted `profiles` list; the identity domain is `ve-<kind>-v0.2:<sorted profiles>`. Each
   new route gets its own gate theorem; the existing gates are unchanged.
4. **Sugar is input-only for strict text.** The canonical strict printer never changes for existing ASTs. Sugar
   (`iff`, "every x that is P", "at least 3", dates, decimals) is accepted on input and expanded before identity.
   The English and fluent views show sugar only when the environment's profile list enables that profile, so
   every existing environment renders byte-identically and `conformance/english-surface-report.json` does not move.
5. **Background premises invalidate models.** Every premise generated from declarations or from a query (flags,
   definitions, literal distinctness, fixed profile premises) is both an implicit premise at every entailment site
   and a validity condition on every finite model at every evaluation, search, equivalence, composition and
   conformance site (section 3.1). Lean and Rust regenerate them from the decoded declarations and query; a list
   supplied on the wire is never trusted.
6. **100% span accounting.** A document's units partition its source; `unformalised(reason)` is a unit kind, never
   an omission. Coverage sums to the source length.
7. **Three-valued results and vacuity.** Every undecidable check (entailment, norm conflict, premise consistency)
   reports YES with a certificate, NO with a checked witness, or UNKNOWN with the exhausted budget. Every document
   check reports whether its premise set has a satisfying finite model (model id), is proved inconsistent
   (certificate; dependent certificates are flagged `vacuous`), or UNKNOWN.
8. **Every construct is authorable.** A construct is offered by `ve assist` only after passing its W8 fidelity
   gate; until then it is "strict only" and listed as such.
9. **Lean wording.** `lean/audit.py:12` scans raw source text, so Lean files and comments say "profile premises"
   or "background premises" and never use the forbidden tokens, even in prose.
10. **Gates.** Spec section reviewed by Astra + Fable xhigh before build; cross-family code review before merge;
    full suite green; `lean/audit.py` allowlist unchanged (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`); no
    `sorry`; identity and parser fixtures byte-identical; an end-to-end Lean acceptance theorem for every enabled
    route; each new theorem listed in plain words in the profile's `.md`; the ownership matrix (section 5)
    respected; one integration agent merges.

### 3.1 Background premises and model validity

```text
Background(env, query) := axioms(env) ++ literal_premises(query)
  axioms(env)              from declarations, bound into environment_id: functional/total flags (W5), definition
                           biconditionals (W3), fixed profile premises (W1: ∀a. required(a) → permitted(a))
  literal_premises(query)  pairwise distinctness ¬(l1 = l2) of the $string literals occurring in the query
                           (W5 stage 1; at most 64 literals, else ResourceLimit); derived from the query, not stored
  model validity           a FiniteModel is valid only if it satisfies Background: functional/total tables checked
                           structurally, defined predicates computed from their bodies (a supplied table is
                           rejected), two literals never share a denotation
```

Sites, each with a test asserting it consults `Background` and reports `InvalidModel` rather than a truth value:
`ve/evaluate.py` (`_validate_model`, line 163), `ve/search.py` (`check_countermodel` 108, distinguishing-model
search, `_subsets`), `ve/equivalence.py`, `ve/argument.py` (every edge; background leaves appear in the report with
label `background:<generator>`; `ContradictoryPremises` at 519 feeds the vacuity report), `ve/entailment.py`
(`prove_entailment` 515, `check_entailment_certificate` 620), `ve/check.py`, the Lean composed route (3.4), and
Rust `eval.rs`, `search.rs`, `model.rs`. Conformance gains a case family "invalid background model" (aliasing
literals, partial total tables, a supplied table for a defined predicate) expected to be rejected by all three.

### 3.4 The composed Lean route (`lean/VE/Route/`)

```text
structure Layer (E : Environment) where
  elaborate : Doc → List Formula                       -- executable, mirrored in Python
  Intended  : Doc → Structure → Prop                   -- the reviewed statement of meaning
  faithful  : ∀ d S, (∀ a ∈ elaborate d, a.denote S []) ↔ Intended d S   -- over structures admissible for the layer below
route     := data ∘ definitions ∘ norms, fixed order: data premises constrain base symbols; definitions are
             conservative over base+data; norms are a definitional layer over base+data+definitions (stratified)
checker   := any `valid` with `CertificateSound E valid` (lean/VE/ClaimGraph.lean:41): today `tableauValid` and
             the closure checker; W4's unified checker later, without rewriting W1 or W3
acceptance theorem per enabled route: an accepted certificate ⇒ Entails E (Background ++ elaborate Γ) (elaborate φ)
             ⇒ (faithfulness, layer by layer) the document-level statement holds in every intended structure
             satisfying the premises
```

`lean/Main.lean` gains one subcommand `route` that reads the envelope's profiles; one file per layer; the v0,
closure and order routes are untouched. Each layer's `Intended` gets a statement review (rule 1b).

### 3.5 Claim labels (provenance that survives citation and composition)

`Graph.Claim` gains `label : String` (`asserted`, `bridge:accepts(<agent>,<unit>)`, `background:<generator>`,
`cite:<bundle>/<lemma>`, `open:<term>`, `readings:<set>@<version>`), part of `DecidableEq`, carried through
`Report.assumptions` and `Unfolds` unchanged, and part of the wire hash. The wire omits the default label so every
existing graph artefact hashes identically (test). Python `check_argument` mirrors it. Admission rule for attributed
and hedged content: the quoted content never appears as a graph node except as a bridge leaf whose label names the
unit; using content without a bridge is therefore structurally impossible, and stripping a label changes the
report and the hash. `lean/METATHEORY.md` is updated; theorem names and meanings are unchanged (they quantify over
`Claim`).

### 3.6 Vacuity report

`ve check`, `ve document check`, `check_argument` and assist `status` report
`premises: model <id> | INCONSISTENT(<certificate>) | UNKNOWN(<budget>)`; a certificate whose premise set is
proved inconsistent is reported `vacuous` and never counted as coverage.

## 4. Workstreams, in build order

### W2. Documents: format, 100% span accounting, content kinds, open terms, reading sets, pilots

**Goal.** Turn a whole document into a checked spine with an explicit residual, measured after every merge.

**Deliverables.** Format `ve-document@1` (`.ve.md`: JSON front matter with environment, units and open terms;
fenced `ve` blocks; the prose between them is the source and is spanned). Unit schema below. Open-term schema.
Reading sets and narrowings. CLI `ve document check|render|coverage|readings`. English rendering of units
("According to the Fed's statement, ..."; "Rule 3. ..."; "This sentence could mean (a) ... or (b) ..."). Coverage
dashboard `tools/coverage_dashboard.py` writing the ROADMAP table. Pilots: US Constitution Art. I s7 (clauses 1-3),
one norm-heavy Claude-constitution section, the Reuters Fed article; each as `usecases/<doc>/commitments.ve.md`
with `coverage.json`, baseline now with today's language, re-measured after each merge, second rater on labels,
blind cross-family fidelity audit.

**Unit schema.**

```text
unit := span [start, end) + kind + meaning ∈ {specified, contextual(context), open(terms), unformalised(reason)}
        + support ∈ {assumption, certificate, observation, calculation, statistical, testimony, adapter, none}
        + content (a VE formula, a norm, a definition, a reading set, or none)
kind := asserted | norm (W1) | definition (W3) | attributed(agent, act ∈ {says, promised, believes, intends, expects, denies})
      | hedged(modality ∈ {could, likely, generally, typically, effectively}) | causal(kind ∈ {because, in order to, prevents})
      | question | instruction | example | exposition | analogy | explanation | meta | unformalised(reason code)
open term := name, arguments, decided_by, necessary conditions (VE), sufficient conditions (VE, optional), factors,
             precedents (meaning ids of pinned facts), jurisdiction, version
partition: units sorted, non-overlapping, contiguous modulo whitespace and punctuation; the checker verifies it
residual  := open terms + assumptions + bridge leaves + unresolved reading sets (the headline number)
```

Attributed and hedged content is quoted by `meaning_id`; the checker verifies the quoted formula is the displayed
one and never infers content from attribution. Using such content in a deduction needs a bridge leaf
(`bridge:accepts(agent, unit)`), which is a labelled assumption (3.5).

**Reading sets (VISION.md section 3).**

```text
readings unit := span + readings [r1..rk] (k ≥ 1; each a unit content with its own meaning_id)
                 + admission per reading: (who, why, distinguishing model id versus each other reading)
                 + status ∈ {authored (k = 1, signed by the author; rejected readings kept as history), open}
                 + version; the set is "readings considered so far"; anyone may propose an extension
narrowing unit := cites (set id, version), keeps ⊆ readings, by whom, reason (cited spans); never edits the set
weights unit   := optional, attributed, removable; ignored by every check; never in the evidence layer
checking a conclusion C whose dependency cone contains ambiguous units U1..Un (readings R_i1..R_ik_i):
  ALL     Γ_all := { ⋁_j R_ij : i } ⊨ C   one certificate; Lean `readings_all_iff`: S ⊨ ⋀_i ⋁_j R_ij ↔ ∃ choice c, S ⊨ ⋀_i R_i,c(i)
  SOME    a certificate for a named choice c, plus a checked countermodel for some other choice c' (else ALL or UNKNOWN)
  NONE    one finite model M with M ⊨ R_ij for all i, j and M ⊭ C, or a countermodel per choice when choices ≤ 64
  UNKNOWN otherwise (budget); k^n is never enumerated for ALL or for the universal NONE witness
  a choice is global per unit within one argument; results under a narrowing are labelled with it
  a cited lemma carries `readings:<set>@<version>` leaves; a later version marks dependants STALE until re-proved
```

**Trusted core.** None in the checker. Lean statement layer: `readings_all_iff` (small) in `lean/VE/Route/Readings.lean`;
the label field (P0). Statement review of the unit schema, the open-term schema and the reading-set rules.

**Depends on / files.** P0 (labels, vacuity). Owns `ve/document.py`, `ve/readings.py`, `ve/openterms.py`,
`ve/surface/registers.py`, `usecases/*/commitments.ve.md`, `tools/coverage_dashboard.py`, `docs/spec/document.md`,
`lean/VE/Route/Readings.lean`. Assist tools (`add_unit`, `add_reading`, `narrow`) are specified here and built by W8.

**Acceptance.** (1) Schema and identity tests; the partition check rejects overlapping, missing and out-of-order
spans. (2) Three pilots with 100% of source bytes in units (whitespace and punctuation excepted); the residual
listed by kind with reason codes; the count of open terms per document reported. (3) Every attributed or hedged
content parses and type-checks; a graph that references quoted content without a bridge leaf is rejected;
stripping a bridge or background label changes the report and the wire hash (tamper test). (4) Coverage table at
baseline, then after each merge; second-rater agreement on kinds reported. (5) Blind fidelity audit of the English
rendering against the source; the disagreement rate is a headline number. (6) Reading sets: a translated paragraph
with three ambiguous sentences shows ALL, SOME and NONE each with its certificate or witness; 12 ambiguous units
with two readings each check ALL with one certificate and no enumeration. (7) A narrowing is recorded and every
result under it is labelled; removing a weights unit changes no verdict. (8) `ve assist` authors a unit and a
reading set end to end (with W8).

**Effort and model.** Format, CLI, rendering 3 days (Sol); pilot authoring 3-4 days (Opus 5.5 through `ve assist`,
no paid API); dashboard 1 day (Sol); Lean readings theorem 1 day (Opus); write-up by Codex.

### W3. Definitions, lemma citation and statement-layer sugar

**Goal.** Name a property once and use it everywhere; cite a certified lemma by `meaning_id`; write `iff` and
"every point that is prime". Closes math gaps G4, G7, G8 and the constitution's name-versus-content gap.

**Deliverables.** Profile `definitions@0`: vocabulary entry `{"defined": {"arguments": [...], "body": AST}}`;
strict `define p(x) as (φ).`; English "call a point prime if ...". Lemma bundles: a DAG of
`(environment_id, premises, conclusion, certificate, leaf labels)` with a `cite` premise form; `ve bundle check`;
English "by Theorem 3". Sugar `iff`, "every s x that is P", "no s is P" (input-only; rendered only under
`definitions@0`). Versioned definitions: a redefinition is a new vocabulary version; documents pin the version.
Lean: `Definitions` layer (`expand`, `conservative`, `faithful`); the route acceptance theorem instantiated for
definitions. Math mini-paper re-run.

**Semantics.**

```text
definition p(x̄) := φ(x̄)    p new; φ over earlier symbols only (acyclic order); no recursion; closed under x̄
                            p may not carry W5 flags (functional/total) and may not be regulated by W1 norms
Σ+D structures := those satisfying ∀x̄. p(x̄) ↔ φ(x̄); elaboration = these biconditionals as background premises
conservativity : every Σ-structure satisfying the data premises expands uniquely to Σ+D, so Σ+D ⊨ ψ iff Σ ⊨ ψ for ψ over Σ
citation       : a deduction may add lemma L's conclusion iff L is in the same bundle, strictly earlier in the DAG, has
                 the same environment_id (hence the same definitions version), and every hypothesis of L is among the
                 citing deduction's premises or itself cited; the citing report inherits every transitive undischarged
                 leaf of L with its label (bridge, background, open, readings); the bundle is re-checked in one Lean run
```

**Trusted core.** Checker unchanged (definitions stay premises; the `define-unfold@0` optimisation rule is deferred).
New Lean: `Definitions.expand`, `conservative`, `faithful`; citation soundness through the parametric route
(`entails_cut`, `lean/VE/Consequence.lean:37`) rather than a per-checker rewrite. Statement review of the
citation rule.

**Depends on / files.** P0 (`Background`, labels, registry). Owns `ve/definitions.py`, `ve/bundle.py`,
`ve/surface/definitions.py`, `lean/VE/Definitions/*`, `usecases/math-mini-paper`; edits `ve/environment.py` only
through the P0 declaration-extension hook and `ve/argument.py` only for `cite` (patch reviewed by P0's owner).

**Acceptance.** (1) `conservative` and `faithful` audited; a definition mentioning its own symbol, a later symbol
or a free variable is rejected in Python and Lean with the same diagnostic; a flag on a defined predicate is
rejected. (2) Math paper: D2-D5 become definitions with zero copied axiom statements in theorem hypotheses; T7mono

### W1. Norms: obligations, prohibitions, permissions, exceptions and priorities (narrowed)

**Goal.** Write "must", "must not", "may", "unless", "except when", "takes precedence over", "by default" as
numbered rules and have the checker decide what a policy requires in a situation, and whether rules conflict, with
a witness situation when they do.

**Deliverables.** Profile `norms@0`. Norm statements in strict and English surfaces as numbered rule blocks
("Rule 3. ...", "Exception to rule 3: ...", "Rule 5 takes precedence over rule 2."), never inline `unless` chains.
Elaboration to FOL (Python mirror plus the Lean `Norms` layer). Static conflict check, three-valued, with the
witness rendered as a situation (W8). Cedar policy-set adapter (permit, forbid, default deny, missing-attribute
and error behaviour). Reformalised 14th-s1 clauses 2-4 and the Claude-constitution priority rule. Spec
`docs/spec/norms.md` (reviewed before build), Lean `lean/NORMS.md`.

**Semantics.**

```text
norm N   := number; guard G_N(x̄) (a VE formula over the situation variables x̄; regulated predicates only of strictly
            lower stratum); consequence C_N(x̄) = one literal p(x̄) or ¬p(x̄) over a regulated predicate p;
            exceptions E_1..E_k (norms on the same p and x̄); "N' takes precedence over N" makes N' an exception of N
            (same p; the precedence graph must be acyclic: cycles are rejected)
yields_N(x̄)  := G_N(x̄) ∨ ⋁_i yields_{E_i}(x̄)        some norm in N's subtree applies (Catala: the subtree yields a value)
selects_N(x̄) := G_N(x̄) ∧ ¬⋁_i yields_{E_i}(x̄)        N's own consequence is the selected one
elab(N)      := ∀x̄. selects_N(x̄) → C_N(x̄)            for every node of every tree (inlined; trees are finite)
completion   := one per dual pair, declared per predicate: deny-by-default  ∀x̄. p(x̄) → ⋁_{N: C_N = p} selects_N(x̄)
                or allow-by-default ∀x̄. ¬p(x̄) → ⋁_{N: C_N = ¬p} selects_N(x̄); never both
duals        := forbidden(a) is the W3 definition ¬permitted(a), never a second regulated predicate;
                violation(a) := required(a) ∧ ¬done(a) is a W3 definition
profile premise := ∀a. required(a) → permitted(a)  (background, identity-bound)
stratification  := a declared order on regulated predicates; a p-norm's guard (after definitions are expanded)
                   mentions only regulated predicates below p; violations are rejected
conflict(x̄)  := selects_M(x̄) ∧ selects_M'(x̄) with C_M = ¬C_M', or selects required(a) with selects ¬permitted(a);
                checked over guards only, independently of the consequences; two selecting nodes with the same
                consequence are a redundancy warning, not a conflict (a stated deviation from Catala's error)
status       := CONFLICT-FREE (certificate) | CONFLICT (checked witness situation) | UNKNOWN (budget)
decidable fragment := guards quantifier-free and closure-free over x̄ and constants: conflict search is complete
                   (finite models of size ≤ |constants| + |x̄|); outside it the status is three-valued
Cedar        := permit norms conclude permitted(r) for the reified request r; forbid norms conclude ¬permitted(r) and
                take precedence over all permits; deny-by-default completion; each policy condition c is translated
                to (def_c, val_c) by Cedar's error rules (`has`, short-circuit `&&`/`||`, missing attribute = error)
                and the guard is def_c ∧ val_c, so an erroneous policy is inapplicable, as Cedar skips it
```

### W6. Quantities in documents: dates, scaled decimals, counting sugar and finite traces on top of order@0

**Goal.** "On 16 September 2026", "within ten days, Sundays excepted", "3.75%", "16 of the 18 policymakers" as

**Deliverables.** Date literals (ISO date to day number, proleptic Gregorian) and scaled decimals (percent as basis

with stated endpoint conventions. Event-sort and time-indexed relation conventions (`occurs_at(e, t)`; with W4,
`value(signal, t, v)`). Finite traces: a declared horizon `H` per document; `always` and `eventually` as bounded
quantification over `[0, H]`. Counting sugar for literal `n ≤ 32`: "at least n", "exactly n", "at most n",
"n of the m S are P". The excluded-day rule as a weekday case-split elaboration. Re-rated news article and FRET
sample; Art. I s7 timing clause. Evaluation-only `count@0` is dropped this round (section 7).

**Semantics.**

```text

"within n days of d"   d ≤ t ∧ t ≤ d + n (both ends inclusive);  "before d"  t < d;  "no later than d"  t ≤ d;  "after d"  t > d
"Sunday(t)"            ∃q. t = 7q + k_sunday (integer quantifier, from numbers2)
"n days, Sundays excepted, from d" ↦ ⋀_{k=0..6} (d ≡ k (mod 7) → deadline = d + off(k)), off(k) computed by the elaborator
                       and proved in Lean equal to the counting definition "the least D with n non-Sundays in (d, D]"
at least n s x, φ      ↦ ∃x1..xn. distinct(x1..xn) ∧ ⋀ φ(xi)   (capture-avoiding: φ's free indices shifted; Lean `expand_atLeast_iff`)
exactly n              ↦ at least n ∧ ¬ at least n+1;   at most n ↦ ¬ at least n+1;   n of the m S are P ↦ exactly m S ∧ exactly n (S ∧ P)
```
