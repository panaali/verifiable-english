Text 1

A delivery robot can take the ramp or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 25 kilograms. If the weight of the parcel is greater than 2 kilograms, taking the ramp leads to a fall.

The robot takes the ramp with a parcel that weighs 3 kilograms. What does the text tell you?
(a) A fall happens.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A fall might happen or might not: the text only says that a fall needs a parcel heavier than 2 kilograms.
(d) No fall happens: a robot easily carries a 3-kilogram parcel on a ramp.
