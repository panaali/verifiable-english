Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 30 kilograms. Taking the lift leads to a lift failure if the weight of the parcel is greater than 28 kilograms. Taking the lift might lead to a lift failure.

According to the text, how likely is a lift failure if the robot takes the lift?
(a) It happens in every case the text allows.
(b) It cannot happen.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) More likely than not.

Text 2

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. An agent can run the job or run the job with a memory limit. Running the job with a memory limit has a less severe worst case than running the job.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) Running the job with a memory limit is less likely to cause harm than running the job.
(b) The text leaves possible a harm from running the job that is more severe than every harm it leaves possible from running the job with a memory limit.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) Running the job with a memory limit leads to fewer harms than running the job.

Text 3

The actions available to the agent are running the job with a memory limit and doing nothing. Running the job with a memory limit might prevent task abandonment.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) Running the job with a memory limit leads to task abandonment in every case the text allows.
(b) In at least one case the text allows, running the job with a memory limit avoids task abandonment, and doing nothing leads to task abandonment in every case the text allows.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) If the agent runs the job with a memory limit, task abandonment happens in no case the text allows.

Text 4

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 20 kilograms. Taking the lift leads to a lift failure exactly when the weight of the parcel is greater than 3 kilograms.

The robot takes the lift with a parcel that weighs 4 kilograms. What does the text tell you?
(a) No lift failure happens: a lift easily carries 4 kilograms.
(b) A lift failure might happen or might not: the text only says that a lift failure needs a parcel heavier than 3 kilograms.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) A lift failure happens.

Text 5

An agent can run the job with a memory limit or do nothing. The peak memory of the job is at least 2 gigabytes. The peak memory of the job is at most 12 gigabytes. Running the job with a memory limit leads to a job failure if the peak memory of the job is greater than 4 gigabytes.

The agent runs the job with a memory limit, and a job failure happens. According to the text, was the peak memory of the job greater than 4 gigabytes?
(a) Yes: a job failure needs a peak memory greater than 4 gigabytes.
(b) No: a job failure needs a peak memory of at most 4 gigabytes.
(c) The text does not say.

Text 6

A delivery robot can take the stairs or take the lift. Taking the lift cannot lead to a lift failure.

What does the second sentence tell you?
(a) If the robot takes the lift, no lift failure happens in any case the text allows.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A lift failure is unlikely if the robot takes the lift.
(d) The robot is not allowed to cause a lift failure.

Text 7

The actions available to the robot are crossing the road at the crossing and crossing the road between parked cars.
Rule 1. Every action must not be performed, if that action might lead to a collision, unless a staff member has approved that action.
Crossing the road between parked cars cannot lead to a collision.
Rule 2. Every action may be performed, if a staff member has approved that action.

No staff member has approved anything. Does Rule 1 forbid the robot to cross the road between parked cars?
(a) Yes: crossing between parked cars might lead to a collision, and the sentence about it only tells the robot not to let a collision happen.
(b) Yes: crossing between parked cars is dangerous.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) No: the text rules out a collision when the robot crosses between parked cars, so Rule 1 does not apply to that action.
