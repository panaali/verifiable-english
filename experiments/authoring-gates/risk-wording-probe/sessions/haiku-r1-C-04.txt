Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

An agent can run the job, run the job with a memory limit, or do nothing. Running the job with a memory limit might lead to a job failure. Running the job with a memory limit leads to a task delay.

The agent runs the job with a memory limit. According to the text, is there any case with no task delay?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) Yes: a task delay, like a job failure, is only not ruled out.
(c) Yes: a task delay usually happens, but not in every case.
(d) No: a task delay happens in every case the text allows.

Text 2

The clinic gives the full dose, and the weight of the patient is 80 kilograms. What does the text tell you about dizziness?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) Dizziness happens.
(c) No dizziness happens.

Text 4

(a) They are equally severe.

Text 7

The actions available to the robot are crossing the road at the crossing and crossing the road between parked cars.
Rule 1. Every action must not be performed, if that action might lead to a collision, unless a staff member has approved that action.
Crossing the road between parked cars cannot lead to a collision.
Rule 2. Every action may be performed, if a staff member has approved that action.

No staff member has approved anything. Does Rule 1 forbid the robot to cross the road between parked cars?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) Yes: crossing between parked cars is dangerous.
(c) No: the text rules out a collision when the robot crosses between parked cars, so Rule 1 does not apply to that action.
(d) Yes: crossing between parked cars might lead to a collision, and the sentence about it only tells the robot not to let a collision happen.
