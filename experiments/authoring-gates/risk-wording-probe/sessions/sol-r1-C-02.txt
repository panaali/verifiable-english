Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

An agent can run the job or do nothing. The peak memory of the job is at least 2 gigabytes. The peak memory of the job is at most 12 gigabytes. Running the job leads to a server outage exactly when the peak memory of the job is greater than 8 gigabytes.

The agent runs the job, and the peak memory of the job is 5 gigabytes. What does the text tell you about a server outage?
(a) No server outage happens.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A server outage happens.

Text 2

An agent can delete the only copy of the database or do nothing. Deleting the only copy of the database might not lead to data loss.

What does the second sentence tell you?
(a) If the agent deletes the only copy, data loss happens in no case the text allows.
(b) If the agent deletes the only copy, data loss happens in every case the text allows.
(c) In at least one case the text allows, the agent deletes the only copy and no data loss happens.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 3

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. A seizure is severe. A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a fall. A fall is serious or worse.

According to the text, is a fall less severe than a seizure?
(a) Yes: a fall is serious, and a seizure is severe.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) No: a fall is severe, like a seizure.

Text 4

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 30 kilograms. Taking the lift leads to a lift failure if the weight of the parcel is greater than 28 kilograms. Taking the lift might lead to a lift failure.

According to the text, how likely is a lift failure if the robot takes the lift?
(a) It cannot happen.
(b) It happens in every case the text allows.
(c) More likely than not.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 5

The actions available to the robot are taking the ramp and taking the stairs. Taking the stairs leads to a fall. A fall is avoidable by the robot.

What can you conclude about taking the ramp?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) Taking the ramp leads to a fall in every case the text allows.
(c) In at least one case the text allows, the robot takes the ramp and no fall happens.
(d) In no case the text allows does taking the ramp lead to a fall.

Text 6

The actions available to the clinic are giving the patient a vaccine and giving the patient nothing. Giving the patient nothing might prevent a fever.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) In at least one case the text allows, giving the patient nothing avoids a fever, and giving the vaccine leads to a fever in every case the text allows.
(c) If the clinic gives the patient nothing, a fever happens in no case the text allows.
(d) Giving the vaccine avoids a fever in at least one case the text allows.

Text 7

An agent can run the job or do nothing. The peak memory of the job is at least 2 gigabytes. The peak memory of the job is at most 12 gigabytes. Running the job cannot lead to a server outage unless the peak memory of the job is greater than 8 gigabytes.

The agent runs the job, and the peak memory of the job is 10 gigabytes. What does the text tell you about a server outage?
(a) No server outage happens.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A server outage happens, because the peak memory of the job is greater than 8 gigabytes.
