Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient drug B. The dose of drug A is at least 10 milligrams. The dose of drug A is at most 50 milligrams. Giving drug A leads to nausea if the dose of drug A is greater than 40 milligrams. Giving drug A might not lead to nausea. Giving drug B cannot lead to nausea.

According to the text, with which drug is nausea ruled out?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) Only drug B.
(c) Both drug A and drug B.
(d) Neither drug.

Text 2

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a rash. Giving drug A certainly leads to drowsiness.

According to the text, what is the difference between a rash and drowsiness if the clinic gives drug A?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) There is no difference: neither is ruled out, and neither is certain.
(c) A rash is possible; drowsiness is what usually happens.
(d) A rash is not ruled out; drowsiness happens in every case the text allows.

Text 3

An agent can restart the web server or do nothing. Restarting the web server cannot lead to a server outage.

According to the text, can a server outage happen if the agent restarts the web server?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) No: in no case the text allows does restarting the web server lead to a server outage.
(c) Yes: a restart takes the server down for a short time.
(d) It can, but the agent is not allowed to let it happen.

Text 4

The actions available to the agent are running the job, running the job with a memory limit and doing nothing. A server outage is avoidable by the agent.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) At least one action available to the agent avoids a server outage in at least one case the text allows.
(b) At least one action available to the agent avoids a server outage in every case the text allows.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) Every action available to the agent avoids a server outage in at least one case the text allows.

Text 5

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. The temperature of the patient is at least 36 degrees. The temperature of the patient is at most 41 degrees. Giving drug A cannot lead to a seizure unless the temperature of the patient is greater than 40 degrees.

The clinic gives drug A, and the temperature of the patient is 38 degrees. What does the text tell you about a seizure?
(a) No seizure happens.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A seizure happens.

Text 6

The actions available to the agent are emptying the cache and doing nothing.
Rule 1. Every action must not be performed, if that action might lead to data loss, unless the user has confirmed that action.
Emptying the cache cannot lead to data loss.
Rule 2. Every action may be performed, if the user has confirmed that action.

In this policy, what is the sentence "Emptying the cache cannot lead to data loss"?
(a) A rule: it forbids the agent to cause data loss by emptying the cache.
(b) A statement about what can happen: if the agent empties the cache, data loss happens in no case the text allows.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) A rule: it permits the agent to empty the cache.

Text 7

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. A seizure is severe. A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a fall. A fall is serious or worse.

According to the text, is a fall less severe than a seizure?
(a) Yes: a fall is serious, and a seizure is severe.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) No: a fall is severe, like a seizure.
