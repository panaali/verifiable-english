Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

The actions available to the agent are running the job, running the job with a memory limit and doing nothing. A server outage is avoidable by the agent.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) At least one action available to the agent avoids a server outage in at least one case the text allows.
(c) At least one action available to the agent avoids a server outage in every case the text allows.
(d) Every action available to the agent avoids a server outage in at least one case the text allows.

Text 2

A delivery robot can take the stairs or take the lift. Taking the lift cannot lead to a lift failure.

What does the second sentence tell you?
(a) If the robot takes the lift, no lift failure happens in any case the text allows.
(b) The robot is not allowed to cause a lift failure.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) A lift failure is unlikely if the robot takes the lift.

Text 3

An agent can run the job or do nothing. The peak memory of the job is at least 2 gigabytes. The peak memory of the job is at most 12 gigabytes. Running the job cannot lead to a server outage unless the peak memory of the job is greater than 8 gigabytes.

The agent runs the job, and the peak memory of the job is 10 gigabytes. What does the text tell you about a server outage?
(a) No server outage happens.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A server outage happens, because the peak memory of the job is greater than 8 gigabytes.

Text 4

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. A delivery robot can cross the road at the crossing or cross the road between parked cars. Crossing the road between parked cars has a less severe worst case than crossing the road at the crossing.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) Crossing between parked cars is less likely to cause harm than crossing at the crossing.
(b) The text leaves possible a harm from crossing at the crossing that is more severe than every harm it leaves possible from crossing between parked cars.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) Crossing between parked cars leads to fewer harms than crossing at the crossing.

Text 5

A clinic can give the patient the full dose or give the patient half the dose. The weight of the patient is at least 40 kilograms. The weight of the patient is at most 100 kilograms. Giving the full dose leads to dizziness if the weight of the patient is less than 50 kilograms.

The clinic gives the full dose, and the weight of the patient is 80 kilograms. What does the text tell you about dizziness?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) Dizziness happens.
(c) No dizziness happens, because the patient does not weigh less than 50 kilograms.

Text 6

A delivery robot can take the stairs or take the lift. The weight of the parcel is at least 2 kilograms. The weight of the parcel is at most 30 kilograms. Taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel if the weight of the parcel is greater than 25 kilograms. Taking the stairs might not lead to a dropped parcel.

According to the text, is a dropped parcel ruled out if the robot takes the stairs?
(a) Yes: in no case the text allows does taking the stairs lead to a dropped parcel.
(b) No: with a parcel heavier than 25 kilograms, taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel, and the text allows such a parcel.
(c) No: taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel in every case the text allows.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 7

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a rash. Giving drug A certainly leads to drowsiness.

According to the text, what is the difference between a rash and drowsiness if the clinic gives drug A?
(a) There is no difference: neither is ruled out, and neither is certain.
(b) A rash is not ruled out; drowsiness happens in every case the text allows.
(c) A rash is possible; drowsiness is what usually happens.
(d) The text does not say.
