Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

The actions available to the agent are emptying the cache and doing nothing.
Rule 1. Every action must not be performed, if that action might lead to a server outage, unless the user has confirmed that action.
Emptying the cache cannot lead to data loss.
Rule 2. Every action may be performed, if the user has confirmed that action.

According to the text, if the agent empties the cache, can data loss happen?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) It can, but the policy forbids the agent to let it happen.
(c) It can, unless the user has confirmed emptying the cache.
(d) No: the text rules out data loss when the agent empties the cache.

Text 2

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 20 kilograms. Taking the lift leads to a lift failure exactly when the weight of the parcel is greater than 3 kilograms.

The robot takes the lift with a parcel that weighs 4 kilograms. What does the text tell you?
(a) A lift failure might happen or might not: the text only says that a lift failure needs a parcel heavier than 3 kilograms.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) No lift failure happens: a lift easily carries 4 kilograms.
(d) A lift failure happens.

Text 3

An agent can restart the web server or do nothing. Restarting the web server cannot lead to a server outage.

According to the text, can a server outage happen if the agent restarts the web server?
(a) Yes: a restart takes the server down for a short time.
(b) It can, but the agent is not allowed to let it happen.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) No: in no case the text allows does restarting the web server lead to a server outage.

Text 4

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. A late delivery is serious or worse. A collision is minor. A delivery robot can cross the road at the crossing or wait. Crossing the road at the crossing might lead to a collision. Waiting might lead to a late delivery.

According to the text, which severities could a late delivery have?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) Serious or severe.
(c) Only minor.
(d) Only serious.

Text 5

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. A delivery robot can cross the road at the crossing or cross the road between parked cars. Crossing the road between parked cars has a less severe worst case than crossing the road at the crossing.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) Crossing between parked cars leads to fewer harms than crossing at the crossing.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) The text leaves possible a harm from crossing at the crossing that is more severe than every harm it leaves possible from crossing between parked cars.
(d) Crossing between parked cars is less likely to cause harm than crossing at the crossing.

Text 6

A delivery robot can take the stairs or take the lift. The weight of the parcel is at least 2 kilograms. The weight of the parcel is at most 30 kilograms. Taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel if the weight of the parcel is greater than 25 kilograms. Taking the stairs might not lead to a dropped parcel.

According to the text, is a dropped parcel ruled out if the robot takes the stairs?
(a) Yes: in no case the text allows does taking the stairs lead to a dropped parcel.
(b) No: taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel in every case the text allows.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) No: with a parcel heavier than 25 kilograms, taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel, and the text allows such a parcel.

Text 7

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a rash. Giving drug A leads to drowsiness.

According to the text, what is the difference between a rash and drowsiness if the clinic gives drug A?
(a) A rash is not ruled out; drowsiness happens in every case the text allows.
(b) A rash is possible; drowsiness is what usually happens.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) There is no difference: neither is ruled out, and neither is certain.
