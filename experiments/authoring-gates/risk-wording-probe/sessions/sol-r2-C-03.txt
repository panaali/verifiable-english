Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a rash. Giving drug A certainly leads to drowsiness.

According to the text, what is the difference between a rash and drowsiness if the clinic gives drug A?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) A rash is not ruled out; drowsiness happens in every case the text allows.
(c) There is no difference: neither is ruled out, and neither is certain.
(d) A rash is possible; drowsiness is what usually happens.

Text 2

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. A delivery robot can cross the road at the crossing or cross the road between parked cars. Crossing the road between parked cars has a less severe worst case than crossing the road at the crossing.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) Crossing between parked cars is less likely to cause harm than crossing at the crossing.
(b) Crossing between parked cars leads to fewer harms than crossing at the crossing.
(c) The text leaves possible a harm from crossing at the crossing that is more severe than every harm it leaves possible from crossing between parked cars.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 3

The actions available to the robot are taking the lift and taking the stairs. Taking the lift allows a dropped parcel to happen.

What can you conclude about taking the stairs?
(a) Taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel in every case the text allows.
(b) In no case the text allows does taking the stairs lead to a dropped parcel.
(c) In at least one case the text allows, the robot takes the stairs and no parcel is dropped.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 4

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. A seizure is severe. A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a fall. A fall is serious or worse.

According to the text, is a fall less severe than a seizure?
(a) No: a fall is severe, like a seizure.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) Yes: a fall is serious, and a seizure is severe.

Text 5

The actions available to the agent are running the job with a memory limit and doing nothing. Running the job with a memory limit prevents task abandonment.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) Running the job with a memory limit leads to task abandonment in every case the text allows.
(b) If the agent runs the job with a memory limit, task abandonment happens in no case the text allows.
(c) In at least one case the text allows, running the job with a memory limit avoids task abandonment, and doing nothing leads to task abandonment in every case the text allows.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 6

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 20 kilograms. Taking the lift leads to a lift failure exactly when the weight of the parcel is greater than 3 kilograms.

The robot takes the lift with a parcel that weighs 4 kilograms. What does the text tell you?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) A lift failure happens.
(c) A lift failure might happen or might not: the text only says that a lift failure needs a parcel heavier than 3 kilograms.
(d) No lift failure happens: a lift easily carries 4 kilograms.
