Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

A clinic can give the patient penicillin or give the patient nothing. Giving the patient penicillin cannot lead to an allergic reaction.

According to the text, can an allergic reaction happen if the clinic gives the patient penicillin?
(a) It can, but the clinic is not allowed to let it happen.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) Yes: some patients are allergic to penicillin.
(d) No: in no case the text allows does giving penicillin lead to an allergic reaction.

Text 2

The actions available to the clinic are giving the patient a vaccine and giving the patient nothing. Giving the patient nothing might prevent a fever.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) In at least one case the text allows, giving the patient nothing avoids a fever, and giving the vaccine leads to a fever in every case the text allows.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) If the clinic gives the patient nothing, a fever happens in no case the text allows.
(d) Giving the vaccine avoids a fever in at least one case the text allows.

Text 3

An agent can run the job or do nothing. Running the job might not lead to a server outage.

What does the second sentence tell you?
(a) If the agent runs the job, a server outage happens in no case the text allows.
(b) A server outage is unlikely if the agent runs the job.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) In at least one case the text allows, the agent runs the job and no server outage happens.

Text 4

The actions available to the robot are taking the lift and taking the stairs. Taking the lift allows a dropped parcel to happen.

What can you conclude about taking the stairs?
(a) In no case the text allows does taking the stairs lead to a dropped parcel.
(b) Taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel in every case the text allows.
(c) In at least one case the text allows, the robot takes the stairs and no parcel is dropped.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 5

What can you conclude about taking the ramp?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) In at least one case the text allows, the robot takes the ramp and no fall happens.
(c) Taking the ramp leads to a fall in every case the text allows.
(d) In no case the text allows does taking the ramp lead to a fall.

Text 6

An agent can read the log file or do nothing. Reading the log file might lead to data loss.

The agent reads the log file. What does the text tell you about data loss?
(a) Data loss is more likely than not.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) No data loss happens: reading a file does not change it.
(d) Data loss is not ruled out.

Text 7

An agent can run the job or do nothing. The peak memory of the job is at least 2 gigabytes. The peak memory of the job is at most 12 gigabytes. Running the job leads to a server outage exactly when the peak memory of the job is greater than 8 gigabytes.

The agent runs the job, and the peak memory of the job is 5 gigabytes. What does the text tell you about a server outage?
(a) A server outage happens.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) No server outage happens.

Text 8

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. Taking the stairs might lead to a fall. Taking the lift leads to a fall.

The robot takes the lift. What does the text tell you about a fall?
(a) A fall usually happens, but not in every case.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A fall happens in every case the text allows.
(d) No fall happens: a robot is safe inside a lift.
