Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 20 kilograms. Taking the lift leads to a lift failure if the weight of the parcel is greater than 3 kilograms.

The robot takes the lift with a parcel that weighs 4 kilograms. What does the text tell you?
(a) A lift failure might happen or might not: the text only says that a lift failure needs a parcel heavier than 3 kilograms.
(b) A lift failure happens.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) No lift failure happens: a lift easily carries 4 kilograms.

Text 2

An agent can run the job or do nothing. Running the job cannot lead to data loss. Running the job might lead to data loss.

Can the last two sentences both be true?
(a) Yes: data loss can happen, but only rarely.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) No: the first rules data loss out, and the second says data loss is not ruled out.
(d) Yes: the first says the agent is not allowed to cause data loss, and the second says data loss can still happen.

Text 3

A delivery robot can take the stairs or take the lift. The weight of the parcel is at least 2 kilograms. The weight of the parcel is at most 30 kilograms. Taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel if the weight of the parcel is greater than 25 kilograms. Taking the stairs might not lead to a dropped parcel.

According to the text, is a dropped parcel ruled out if the robot takes the stairs?
(a) No: with a parcel heavier than 25 kilograms, taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel, and the text allows such a parcel.
(b) No: taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel in every case the text allows.
(c) Yes: in no case the text allows does taking the stairs lead to a dropped parcel.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 4

An agent can run the job or do nothing. The peak memory of the job is at least 2 gigabytes. The peak memory of the job is at most 12 gigabytes. Running the job cannot lead to a server outage unless the peak memory of the job is greater than 8 gigabytes.

The agent runs the job, and the peak memory of the job is 10 gigabytes. What does the text tell you about a server outage?
(a) A server outage happens, because the peak memory of the job is greater than 8 gigabytes.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) No server outage happens.

Text 5

The actions available to the clinic are giving the patient drug A and giving the patient drug B. Giving drug A allows a rash to happen.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) Giving drug A leads to a rash in every case the text allows, and giving drug B avoids a rash in every case the text allows.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) Giving drug A leads to a rash in every case the text allows, and giving drug B avoids a rash in at least one case the text allows.
(d) The clinic is permitted to let a rash happen when it gives drug A.

Text 6

An agent can run the job, run the job with a memory limit, or do nothing. Running the job with a memory limit might lead to a job failure. Running the job with a memory limit leads to a task delay.

The agent runs the job with a memory limit. According to the text, is there any case with no task delay?
(a) Yes: a task delay, like a job failure, is only not ruled out.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) Yes: a task delay usually happens, but not in every case.
(d) No: a task delay happens in every case the text allows.

Text 7

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. An agent can run the job or do nothing. Running the job might lead to a server outage. A server outage is serious or worse.

According to the text, which severities could a server outage have?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) Only serious.
(c) Only severe.
(d) Serious or severe.
