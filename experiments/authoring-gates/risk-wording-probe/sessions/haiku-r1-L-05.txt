Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

Legend:
Each agent faces one choice among the actions available to it and takes at most one of them.
"<action> leads to <harm>" means the harm happens in every case the declared estimates, conditions and statements allow.
"<action> might not lead to <harm>" means some case they allow avoids the harm.
"<action> allows <harm> to happen" means the action leads to the harm, and another action available to the same chooser might not lead to it.

The actions available to the agent are running the job, running the job with a memory limit and doing nothing. Running the job allows a server outage to happen.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) The agent is permitted to let a server outage happen by running the job.
(b) Running the job leads to a server outage in every case the text allows, and at least one other action available to the agent avoids a server outage in at least one case the text allows.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) Running the job leads to a server outage in every case the text allows, and at least one other action available to the agent avoids a server outage in every case the text allows.

Text 2

Legend:
Each robot faces one choice among the actions available to it and takes at most one of them.
"<action> might lead to <harm>" means the declared estimates, conditions and statements do not rule the harm out.
"<action> cannot lead to <harm>" means they rule it out.
By default, an action may be performed.

The actions available to the robot are crossing the road at the crossing and crossing the road between parked cars.
Rule 1. Every action must not be performed, if that action might lead to a collision, unless a staff member has approved that action.
Crossing the road between parked cars cannot lead to a collision.
Rule 2. Every action may be performed, if a staff member has approved that action.

No staff member has approved anything. Does Rule 1 forbid the robot to cross the road between parked cars?
(a) No: the text rules out a collision when the robot crosses between parked cars, so Rule 1 does not apply to that action.
(b) Yes: crossing between parked cars is dangerous.
(c) Yes: crossing between parked cars might lead to a collision, and the sentence about it only tells the robot not to let a collision happen.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 3

Legend:
Each agent faces one choice among the actions available to it and takes at most one of them.
"<action> leads to <harm> exactly when <condition>" means the harm happens in every case the declared estimates, conditions and statements allow in which the condition holds, and in no case they allow in which it does not hold.

An agent can apply the database migration or do nothing. The duration of the migration is at least 5 minutes. The duration of the migration is at most 60 minutes. Applying the migration leads to a task delay exactly when the duration of the migration is greater than 30 minutes.

Which of these follows from the text?
(a) If the agent applies the migration, a task delay can happen only when the migration takes more than 30 minutes, but need not happen then.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) If the agent applies the migration, a task delay happens when the migration takes more than 30 minutes, and in no other case.
(d) If the agent applies the migration, a task delay happens when the migration takes more than 30 minutes; the text does not say what happens when it takes 30 minutes or less.

Text 4

Legend:
Each robot faces one choice among the actions available to it and takes at most one of them.
"<action> might not lead to <harm>" means some case the declared estimates, conditions and statements allow avoids the harm.
"<action> leads to <harm> if <condition>" means the harm happens in every case they allow in which the condition holds.

Taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel if the weight of the parcel is greater than 25 kilograms. Taking the stairs might not lead to a dropped parcel.

According to the text, is a dropped parcel ruled out if the robot takes the stairs?
(a) No: taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel in every case the text allows.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) No: with a parcel heavier than 25 kilograms, taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel, and the text allows such a parcel.
(d) Yes: in no case the text allows does taking the stairs lead to a dropped parcel.
