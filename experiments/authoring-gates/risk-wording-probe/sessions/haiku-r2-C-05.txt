Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

A clinic can give the patient the full dose or give the patient half the dose. The weight of the patient is at least 50 kilograms. The weight of the patient is at most 90 kilograms. Giving the full dose leads to dizziness if the weight of the patient is less than 52 kilograms. Giving the full dose might lead to dizziness.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) Dizziness is not ruled out if the clinic gives the full dose.
(b) Dizziness is likely if the clinic gives the full dose.
(c) Dizziness happens in every case the text allows if the clinic gives the full dose.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 2

An agent can apply the database migration or do nothing. The duration of the migration is at least 5 minutes. The duration of the migration is at most 60 minutes. Applying the migration cannot lead to a task delay unless the duration of the migration is greater than 30 minutes.

The agent applies the migration, and a task delay happens. According to the text, was the duration of the migration greater than 30 minutes?
(a) No: it was 30 minutes or less.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) Yes: it was greater than 30 minutes.

Text 3

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a rash. Giving drug A leads to drowsiness.

According to the text, what is the difference between a rash and drowsiness if the clinic gives drug A?
(a) A rash is not ruled out; drowsiness happens in every case the text allows.
(b) A rash is possible; drowsiness is what usually happens.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) There is no difference: neither is ruled out, and neither is certain.

Text 4

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. Data loss is minor. A task delay is severe. An agent can delete the old logs or do nothing. Deleting the old logs might lead to data loss. Doing nothing might lead to a task delay.

According to the text, which harm is more severe: data loss or a task delay?
(a) Data loss.
(b) A task delay.
(c) They are equally severe.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 5

An agent can run the job or do nothing. The peak memory of the job is at least 2 gigabytes. The peak memory of the job is at most 12 gigabytes. Running the job leads to a server outage if the peak memory of the job is greater than 8 gigabytes.

The agent runs the job, and the peak memory of the job is 5 gigabytes. What does the text tell you about a server outage?
(a) No server outage happens, because the peak memory of the job is not greater than 8 gigabytes.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A server outage happens.

Text 6

The actions available to the agent are running the job, running the job with a memory limit and doing nothing. Running the job allows a server outage to happen.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) Running the job leads to a server outage in every case the text allows, and at least one other action available to the agent avoids a server outage in at least one case the text allows.
(b) Running the job leads to a server outage in every case the text allows, and at least one other action available to the agent avoids a server outage in every case the text allows.
(c) The agent is permitted to let a server outage happen by running the job.
(d) The text does not say.
