Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

Legend:
Each clinic faces one choice among the actions available to it and takes at most one of them.
"<action> might lead to <harm>" means the declared estimates, conditions and statements do not rule the harm out.
"<action> cannot lead to <harm>" means they rule it out.
"<action> might not lead to <harm>" means some case they allow avoids the harm.
A clinic prevents a harm by taking an action that cannot lead to the harm.
By default, an action may be performed.

The actions available to the clinic are giving the patient an antihistamine and giving the patient penicillin. Giving the patient an antihistamine might lead to a rash. Giving the patient an antihistamine might not lead to a rash. Giving the patient penicillin cannot lead to a rash.
Rule 1. Every clinic must prevent a rash.

According to the text, which of the clinic's actions fulfil Rule 1?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) Only giving the patient an antihistamine.
(c) Giving the patient an antihistamine and giving the patient penicillin.
(d) Only giving the patient penicillin.
