Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 20 kilograms. Taking the lift leads to a lift failure if the weight of the parcel is greater than 3 kilograms.

The robot takes the lift with a parcel that weighs 4 kilograms. What does the text tell you?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) A lift failure might happen or might not: the text only says that a lift failure needs a parcel heavier than 3 kilograms.
(c) No lift failure happens: a lift easily carries 4 kilograms.
(d) A lift failure happens.

Text 2

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient drug B. The dose of drug A is at least 10 milligrams. The dose of drug A is at most 50 milligrams. Giving drug A leads to nausea if the dose of drug A is greater than 40 milligrams. Giving drug A might not lead to nausea. Giving drug B cannot lead to nausea.

According to the text, with which drug is nausea ruled out?
(a) Neither drug.
(b) Both drug A and drug B.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) Only drug B.

Text 3

The actions available to the agent are emptying the cache and doing nothing.
Rule 1. Every action must not be performed, if that action might lead to data loss, unless the user has confirmed that action.
Emptying the cache cannot lead to data loss.
Rule 2. Every action may be performed, if the user has confirmed that action.

In this policy, what is the sentence "Emptying the cache cannot lead to data loss"?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) A rule: it permits the agent to empty the cache.
(c) A rule: it forbids the agent to cause data loss by emptying the cache.
(d) A statement about what can happen: if the agent empties the cache, data loss happens in no case the text allows.

Text 4

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. Taking the stairs might lead to a fall. Taking the lift leads to a fall.

The robot takes the lift. What does the text tell you about a fall?
(a) No fall happens: a robot is safe inside a lift.
(b) A fall happens in every case the text allows.
(c) The text does not say.
(d) A fall usually happens, but not in every case.

Text 5

The actions available to the clinic are giving the patient drug A, giving the patient drug B and giving the patient nothing. Giving the patient nothing leads to a fever. Giving drug A leads to a fever. A fever is avoidable by the clinic.

What can you conclude about giving drug B?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) In at least one case the text allows, the clinic gives drug B and no fever happens.
(c) Giving drug B leads to a fever in every case the text allows.
(d) In no case the text allows does giving drug B lead to a fever.

Text 6

The actions available to the clinic are giving the patient a vaccine and giving the patient nothing. Giving the patient nothing might prevent a fever.

What does the last sentence tell you?
(a) In at least one case the text allows, giving the patient nothing avoids a fever, and giving the vaccine leads to a fever in every case the text allows.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) If the clinic gives the patient nothing, a fever happens in no case the text allows.
(d) Giving the vaccine avoids a fever in at least one case the text allows.

Text 7

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. An agent can run the job or do nothing. Running the job might lead to a server outage. A server outage is serious or worse.

According to the text, which severities could a server outage have?
(a) Serious or severe.
(b) Only severe.
(c) Only serious.
(d) The text does not say.
