Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 30 kilograms. Taking the lift leads to a lift failure if the weight of the parcel is greater than 28 kilograms. Taking the lift might lead to a lift failure.

According to the text, how likely is a lift failure if the robot takes the lift?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) It happens in every case the text allows.
(c) More likely than not.
(d) It cannot happen.

Text 2

The actions available to the robot are crossing the road at the crossing and crossing the road between parked cars.
Rule 1. Every action must not be performed, if that action might lead to a collision, unless a staff member has approved that action.
Crossing the road between parked cars cannot lead to a collision.
Rule 2. Every action may be performed, if a staff member has approved that action.

No staff member has approved anything. Does Rule 1 forbid the robot to cross the road between parked cars?
(a) No: the text rules out a collision when the robot crosses between parked cars, so Rule 1 does not apply to that action.
(b) Yes: crossing between parked cars might lead to a collision, and the sentence about it only tells the robot not to let a collision happen.
(c) Yes: crossing between parked cars is dangerous.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 3

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a rash. Giving drug A leads to drowsiness.

According to the text, what is the difference between a rash and drowsiness if the clinic gives drug A?
(a) A rash is possible; drowsiness is what usually happens.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) There is no difference: neither is ruled out, and neither is certain.
(d) A rash is not ruled out; drowsiness happens in every case the text allows.

Text 4

A delivery robot can take the stairs or take the lift. Taking the lift cannot lead to a lift failure.

What does the second sentence tell you?
(a) If the robot takes the lift, no lift failure happens in any case the text allows.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A lift failure is unlikely if the robot takes the lift.
(d) The robot is not allowed to cause a lift failure.

Text 5

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. The temperature of the patient is at least 36 degrees. The temperature of the patient is at most 41 degrees. Giving drug A cannot lead to a seizure unless the temperature of the patient is greater than 40 degrees.

The clinic gives drug A, and the temperature of the patient is 38 degrees. What does the text tell you about a seizure?
(a) The text does not say.
(b) No seizure happens.
(c) A seizure happens.

Text 6

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. A late delivery is serious or worse. A collision is minor. A delivery robot can cross the road at the crossing or wait. Crossing the road at the crossing might lead to a collision. Waiting might lead to a late delivery.

According to the text, which severities could a late delivery have?
(a) Only minor.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) Only serious.
(d) Serious or severe.

Text 7

An agent can delete the only copy of the database or do nothing. Deleting the only copy of the database might not lead to data loss.

What does the second sentence tell you?
(a) If the agent deletes the only copy, data loss happens in no case the text allows.
(b) If the agent deletes the only copy, data loss happens in every case the text allows.
(c) In at least one case the text allows, the agent deletes the only copy and no data loss happens.
