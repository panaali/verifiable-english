Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a rash. Giving drug A leads to drowsiness.

According to the text, what is the difference between a rash and drowsiness if the clinic gives drug A?
(a) There is no difference: neither is ruled out, and neither is certain.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A rash is possible; drowsiness is what usually happens.
(d) A rash is not ruled out; drowsiness happens in every case the text allows.

Text 2

A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. The temperature of the patient is at least 36 degrees. The temperature of the patient is at most 41 degrees. Giving drug A cannot lead to a seizure unless the temperature of the patient is greater than 40 degrees.

The clinic gives drug A, and the temperature of the patient is 38 degrees. What does the text tell you about a seizure?
(a) A seizure happens.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) No seizure happens.

Text 3

A delivery robot can take the lift or take the stairs. The weight of the parcel is at least 1 kilogram. The weight of the parcel is at most 20 kilograms. Taking the lift leads to a lift failure exactly when the weight of the parcel is greater than 3 kilograms.

The robot takes the lift with a parcel that weighs 4 kilograms. What does the text tell you?
(a) No lift failure happens: a lift easily carries 4 kilograms.
(b) A lift failure might happen or might not: the text only says that a lift failure needs a parcel heavier than 3 kilograms.
(c) A lift failure happens.
(d) The text does not say.

Text 4

Severity, from most to least severe: severe, serious, minor. A seizure is severe. A clinic can give the patient drug A or give the patient nothing. Giving drug A might lead to a fall. A fall is serious or worse.

According to the text, is a fall less severe than a seizure?
(a) No: a fall is severe, like a seizure.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) Yes: a fall is serious, and a seizure is severe.

Text 5

The actions available to the robot are taking the ramp and taking the stairs. Taking the stairs leads to a fall. A fall is avoidable by the robot.

What can you conclude about taking the ramp?
(a) Taking the ramp leads to a fall in every case the text allows.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) In no case the text allows does taking the ramp lead to a fall.
(d) In at least one case the text allows, the robot takes the ramp and no fall happens.

Text 6

An agent can run the job, run the job with a memory limit, or do nothing. Running the job might lead to a server outage.

What does the second sentence tell you?
(a) The text does not rule out a server outage if the agent runs the job.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) A server outage is likely if the agent runs the job.
(d) A server outage happens in every case the text allows if the agent runs the job.

Text 7

The actions available to the robot are taking the lift and taking the stairs. Taking the lift allows a dropped parcel to happen.

What can you conclude about taking the stairs?
(a) Taking the stairs leads to a dropped parcel in every case the text allows.
(b) In no case the text allows does taking the stairs lead to a dropped parcel.
(c) In at least one case the text allows, the robot takes the stairs and no parcel is dropped.
(d) The text does not say.
