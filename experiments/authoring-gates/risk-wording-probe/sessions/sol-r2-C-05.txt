Read each short text and answer its question from the text alone. Choose one option and give a one-sentence reason.

Text 1

The agent runs the job with a memory limit, and a job failure happens. According to the text, was the peak memory of the job greater than 4 gigabytes?
(a) No: a job failure needs a peak memory of at most 4 gigabytes.
(b) Yes: a job failure needs a peak memory greater than 4 gigabytes.
(c) The text does not say.

Text 2

(a) No dizziness happens.
(b) The text does not say.
(c) Dizziness happens.
