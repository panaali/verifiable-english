## Notes (hand-written after scoring; they change no decision)

- **Run.** All 56 sessions ran once, on 2026-09-29, each in a fresh empty directory under `/tmp`: 28 Claude Haiku 4.5
  (`claude -p --tools ""`) and 28 GPT-6 Sol (`codex exec -s read-only`). There were no transport errors and no
  retries. No Sol session used a tool (`tool_items` is empty in every `meta.json`), and every temporary directory was
  still empty afterwards. All 408 answers parsed; none needed the option-text fallback. The sealed files are
  unchanged since the seal commit (`MANIFEST.sha256` paths are relative to this directory).
- **Items found defective after sealing:** none. c5-1, the item behind the one critical L answer, was rechecked: its
  key (c, "The text does not say") follows from the sufficient-only reading, and the critical option (a) is the
  "only if" reading the reader applied ("the condition for the outage is not met").
- **c5 fails on one answer.** 15/16 in L, but that one wrong answer is critical (haiku-r1-L-06 on c5-1), and rule 1
  allows no critical answer. Sol was 8/8 and C was 16/16. By rule 4, `leads to … if` is pending wording, and the
  two reviewers propose one replacement, to be probed once with new sealed items.
- **c10 is legend-dependent.** It passes L 16/16, but in C sol-r2-C-02 read "avoidable" as "some action avoids it in
  every case" (critical, c10-1). Item 1 marks it, and W8 re-tests it.
- **c11 and c12 work only with the legend (observation; not a rule-defined status).** In L they are 16/16 and 8/8.
  In C they are 0/16 and 1/8, but with no critical answers, so under 2.6 they are not legend-dependent. In C, 10
  of the 16 c11 answers chose "The text does not say", and 6 chose the permission option ("is permitted to let … 
  happen"), a deontic reading of `allows` that is not the construct's listed rival. 7 of the 8 c12 answers chose
  "The text does not say". The reviewers may want item 1 to record that these words carry their risk meaning only
  through the legend.
- **`prevents` control.** 7 of 8 answers took the ordinary-English guarantee reading, in L as well as C. The eighth
  (haiku-r2-L-01) chose "The text does not say" because the legend does not define `prevents`. This supports the
  rejection of bare `prevents`, which stands in any case (rule 5).
