Secondary outcomes: answer accuracy, certified-answer rate, and faithful+premise-bound certified-answer rate, reported separately. The deduction checker sees only the submitted translations, never gold premises. Bind each translated premise to its same-named graph leaf; bind the root to the translated query or its negation. Extra assumption leaves fail grounding. A certificate alone does not establish translation fidelity. Uncertain requires TWO VE-checked finite models satisfying all translated premises, one making the query true and one false; proof-search exhaustion never establishes uncertainty.
