## 1. Contract, migration, and measurable coverage

An English speaker must read and write VE without tutorials, manuals, vocabulary files, or logic notation: type English, answer English questions, edit prose. Universal usability remains unproved. Arbitrary English enters drafts; only uniquely admitted, reviewed meanings enter the checked spine. Exposition and unresolved commitments remain visible and labelled; every source span is accounted for.

**One surface, versioned presentation.** Converge `ve_english` and `ve_natural` at the parser, admission path, and IDE default view, with a versioned grammar, vocabulary, document parser, and renderer. Checked displayed prose must be writable verbatim, preserving wording, paragraphs, and confirmed synonyms in presentation metadata. Legacy view names keep pinned legacy rendering for replay; new documents explicitly select the new presentation version. Existing environments' `ve_english` bytes and `conformance/english-surface-report.json` stay frozen, as do identities, canonical/wire bytes, certificates, routes, and theorem statements. Migration previews new prose and renews presentation approval; unchanged meanings can reuse certificates.

**Explicit EXPAND2 reconciliation.** Rule 4's rendering freeze remains intact: new rendering is opt-in/versioned, not replacement of legacy output. Section 3.2's strict fallback is amended for the ordinary IDE: exhausted admission leads to English repair or an unresolved unit. Strict syntax remains an advanced inspector and a recorded coverage failure. Exactly one derivation is required even when competing derivations yield the same AST; make this interpretation explicit and gate it on legacy fixture replay (§5).

**Mandatory guarantees.** Preserve exact scope, mechanical checking, the small Lean-verified checker, Python/Rust/Lean agreement within their declared scopes, replayable certificates, validated countermodels, and honest UNKNOWN. Bind assumptions, profiles, definitions, and dependencies to identities; report inconsistency/vacuity separately. Checking proves conditional structure, never empirical truth, source fidelity, intent, or open-term adjudication. Elaborate into existing profiles with independently specified semantics and Lean faithfulness/model correspondence. New profiles require a committed pilot's failed elaboration or budgeted inference attempt, a new route, conservativity, certificate soundness at every universe, executable model validity, verified composition bridges, statement review, axiom audit, conformance, and a trust-cost entry. Judgments stay context-free; sealed non-monotone and sibling results retain kinds/assumptions through verified bridges. Reviewed pinned Lean imports are labelled premises.

**Baseline and repair scope.** Natural-view `92ba2b8`: 599/1,219 natural samples (49.1%); use cases 73/96 (76.0%); 620 fallbacks. Its zero “accepted wrong readings” means **zero AST round-trip mismatches**, not measured source fidelity or comprehension. Of the fallbacks, 618 contain structural notation; two fail AST/audit checks. Z1 repairs nested compounds with explicit English scope, multiple binders with typed ordinal noun phrases, and quoted identifiers with confirmed display names now. Unbounded nesting, unresolved name collisions, and wordings without faithful parser correspondence remain deferred residuals. Gate the fixed 1,219-sample cohort at ≥90% notation-free round trips, zero mismatches, and a disposition for all 620 fallbacks; clause/ordinal scaffolding still needs comprehension testing.

**Document measures.** W2's law, constitution-policy, and news pilots are development; seal complete procedure, contract, and technical excerpts as held-out before tuning. Pin source/version/hash/licence, boundaries, commitment units, reference meanings, admissible readings, and residuals independently of grammar-fitting authors. Label paraphrases; obtain licensed verbatim snapshots before original-document claims. Generated ASTs remain a separate stress cohort. Let **U** be all sealed commitment units, **R** units preserving every reference reading, force, scope and dependency, **N** represented units writable/rendered without `@`, brackets, operators or variable letters. Publish R/U, N/R (N/A if R=0), and N/U; splitting cannot inflate success. Require 100% span accounting, N/U ≥95% development and ≥90% held-out, zero accepted fidelity errors. Publish open terms, assumptions, bridges, unresolved readings, direct/repair counts, and fallback reasons. Comprehension and completion have separate gates (§5).

## 2. Recovery and deterministic alternatives

CLI `ve assist` and the blank-document IDE share one service/ledger. Classify units by parse outcome (zero/one/multiple/exhausted), vocabulary, discourse completeness, unsupported force, and check status. Unique parsing never resolves intent. Retain source throughout these required transitions:

| State / trigger | English interaction and next state |
|---|---|
| Draft → analysing | Type/paste; run full grammar/catalogue with pinned context. Cancel stops work and returns to draft without acceptance. |
| Needs word / zero parse | Highlight unknown term or longest accepted prefix, expected construction, and three nearest registered wordings (stable token-edit ranking). Prefixes are diagnostic, never partial admission. Define/edit/template → analysing. |
| Needs repair / unsupported | Explain rejection. “None of these means what I intended” retains rejected candidates; request an English restatement/example, referent, or supported split. Edit/template/proposer → analysing; no faithful repair → unresolved with reason. |
| Reading review / pending distinction | Separate validated cards from pending alternatives; pending blocks approval. Edit/select → full reparse/correspondence. Never present unverified distinctions as proved or discard them silently. |
| Ready → approved | Author confirms meaning, paraphrase, checked situation and presentation. Persist approval; recheck dependants. Proof is not intent approval. Translation follows §3. |
| Exhausted / proposer unavailable | Name budget/outage; offer deterministic prompts, save, cancel, or unresolved. No automatic retry. Explicit retry logs a new attempt without erasing evaluation failure. |
| Stale / unresolved | Edits invalidate affected approvals/dependants; reanalyse. Unresolved is visible/resumable and blocks dependent certification. Undo creates a revision and revalidates restored approvals. |

**Catalogue, not implied completeness.** Z3/W8 ships a deterministic versioned catalogue: stable pattern ID, token/discourse matcher, preconditions, alternative AST constructors, writable paraphrase templates, and one sealed fixture plus negative/boundary cases per pattern. Match source even with a unique parse. Initial classes: universal/existential and negation scope, coordination/list grouping and collective/distributive readings, modifier/relative attachment, discourse reference, narrow predicate ellipsis. Construct only profile-supported meanings; unsupported alternatives remain residuals. These are “patterns considered so far”, open to challengers, not exhaustive English interpretation.

Use existing `find_distinguishing_model`/comparison machinery; independently validate models against regenerated Background and evaluate both truth values. Fire a distinct-reading card only with a checked witness of non-equivalence; one separating direction suffices. Merge equivalents only with checked equivalence certificates. No model found, incomparable backgrounds, or exhaustion means pending/UNKNOWN, not equivalence or permission to drop an alternative. Distinct reading sets need pairwise distinctions; all candidate paraphrases/rewrite selections pass the full parser and AST correspondence before adoption.

**Limits v1.** Stable pattern-ID/constructor ordering; ≤32 candidate ASTs/unit, 496 pair comparisons, three cards/page. Record counts/continuation cursor; overflow leaves an incomplete, unresolved set. Each comparison: domain sizes 0–2, ≤10,000 models, ≤1,000,000 evaluations; an eight-second operation deadline also bounds grammar/search. Name whichever limit fires, including MAX_NODES/Earley; bind limits/versions in the ledger.

**Honest repair dependence.** Proposer-free diagnostics, vocabulary prompts, catalogue templates, and user alternatives cover limited constructions. Arbitrary zero-parse English may require a proposer and may still fail (P5: 0/12 one-shot; P3: fidelity after repair). Proposers are untrusted suggestions. The web branch uses Haiku per click, capped at 30 calls/day: retain that ceiling, reserve before calls, expose availability. Task cap: eight clarification replies, five minutes, two proposer requests (50-second request deadline); then save unresolved. Measure free/assisted completion separately on identical sealed tasks (§5), including failed repairs/outages.

## 3. Meaning, glossary, and discourse

**Approval and translation.** Record source anchor, considered/rejected readings, paraphrase, situation/model, selected meaning, actor, and grammar/environment/glossary/catalogue/reading-set versions. Approval binds meaning identity and source/presentation snapshot; edits invalidate it and dependants, while unchanged meaning may reuse its certificate. Mode is per unit. Authoring selects one reading and retains rejection history. Translation preserves source alternatives/provenance; an attributed, cited narrowing is a separate unit, never deletion. Probabilities are removable annotations outside checking. Show ALL/SOME/NONE/UNKNOWN and each narrowing dependency. W2's disjunction/shared-countermodel shortcuts apply only to assertion readings with identical backgrounds; otherwise elaborate complete choices separately, up to 64, then UNKNOWN. Choices are global per unit; incompatible citations reject; expanded sets stale results. No UI action silently collapses a translation set.

**Glossary dialogue.** Seed from reviewed declarations/display overlays. Separate concept identity from spelling and preserve each occurrence's wording. “In this document, staff, employees and personnel mean the same thing” creates confirmed conservative W3 aliases with matching argument types/direction, never global replacement. Reject cycles, collisions, arity conflicts, and reversed arguments. Glossary revisions change environment bindings and trigger rechecking; cross-profile aliases wait for Lean admission/conservativity/model validity and Rust parity. In authoring ask whether “personnel” means staff or a different group; in translation cite explicit source synonymy, otherwise keep same/different readings and disclose narrowing dependence.

WordNet (`ve/lexicon/oewn-2025.json`, attribution, `tools/build_lexicon.py`) supplies pinned advisory classes, morphology, and senses. Classes do not settle phrase boundaries; shared senses do not prove synonymy. Keep compatible analyses; dictionary absence cannot exclude technical or multilingual names. New-word prompts ask “a person, a property, or something one person does to another?”, then show positive/negative examples, confirm direction/gloss, and adopt explicitly. Typo suggestions and generated declarations remain pending; no hidden premises or world knowledge.

**Discourse exceeds constants.** Track named, existential, quantified, plural/group, and possessive mentions, accessibility, agreement, and unresolved antecedents. Batch 2's single-constant “it” rule is insufficient: “The API is deployed. Some service is reviewed. It is safe” has another plausible antecedent. If resolution is incomplete, require explicit-name/referent confirmation even with one declared constant or one parse. A quantified/existential referent needs a faithful scoped description/rewrite; never invent a constant or assume identity. Proximity is not consent. Earlier edits rerun resolution and invalidate affected approvals; locally bound relative clauses retain scope.

**Executable meaning cases (Z3/Z4).** Seal the API/service example; plural “they” after a group and another plural mention; “her manager” after Alice and Beth; and “Alice reviews the API; Bob checks the service; Carol does too”. Require all supported antecedents/completions or an explicit incomplete-resolution residual, then a confirmed scoped rewrite or preserved translation alternatives. Ellipsis comes from narrow catalogue predicate-copy constructors first, optional proposer suggestions second; record provenance and confirm each completion. Never silently borrow scope, predicates, or omitted premises.

Vagueness fixture: “Take reasonable precautions” retains an open predicate with deciding role, known necessary/sufficient conditions, pinned examples/precedents, jurisdiction, and version. Test that application awaits that role, adjudications become labelled premises, and no numeric threshold is invented. Causal/hedge fixtures (“The patch caused recovery”; “The service is probably safe”) retain their force or stay unformalised; replacing them by an opaque atom cannot pass fidelity. Splits retain attribution, commitments, dependencies, and original spans.

**Sealed scope example.** “Every engineer checks a server” considers “Each engineer checks at least one server; different engineers may check different servers” and “There is at least one server that every engineer checks.” Alice/Atlas and Bob/Beacon with no common server distinguishes them; a common-server world satisfies both. The second entails the first, so do not demand a reverse witness. Test empty engineer/server domains where permitted. Today's shortening is `owns`-specific: these `checks` rewrites are proposed fixtures, not claimed working syntax; Z1 must admit them before offering cards.

## 5. Acceptance, persistence, and usability evidence

**Deterministic gates.** Reproduce `conformance.natural_surface` after branch integration. Test source → parsed document → prose → parsed document with exact AST, identity, roles, anchors, glossary, and dependencies. Log parse counts and budget/version diagnostics per input; only completed exactly-one-derivation/correspondence checks admit. Replay all 85,000 round-trip/differential pairs and the separate historical 12,750 Earley texts; reject constructed duplicate derivations even with equal ASTs. These are regression gates, not claimed results of this revision. Mix all merged profiles through depth three, whole paragraphs, names, lists, relative scopes, and conclusion markers (deductions, never assumptions). Mutate negation, scope, arguments, antecedents, aliases, units, and approvals; require rejection/invalidation. Run every catalogue fixture with proposer disabled, including equivalent/UNKNOWN/background-invalid comparisons and generation/pagination limits.

**Browser acceptance (mocked proposers; all scenarios must pass).** Start at a blank document without tour, docs, strict syntax, or JSON. (1) Enter unknown vocabulary; define/alias, reject typo, confirm direction, type the scope fixture, inspect checked worlds, rewrite/approve. (2) Enter zero-parse prose; inspect prefix/templates; select “none”, fail repair repeatedly, encounter an unavailable proposer, cancel, undo, resume, exhaust each budget, and finish with a visible reasoned unresolved unit. (3) Exercise every §3 discourse/ellipsis/vague/causal case and translation narrowing; refuse silent collapse and preserve results after weight removal. (4) Edit an earlier referent or glossary, observe stale dependants, resolve again. (5) Reload, restart server, export/import/replay; submit stale edits from two tabs, cancel in-flight work, and tamper with text, anchors, versions, meaning IDs, approvals, and exported events. Assert no lost acknowledged draft, stale approval, hidden residual, or false assurance.

**Persistent ledger design and security gate.** Replace tab-only drafts/ephemeral sessions with server-managed storage using server-generated IDs, transactional append-only revision events, content hashes, pinned manifests, approval bindings, and recoverable snapshots. Acknowledge only durable commits; compare-and-swap revisions reject stale writes; idempotent request IDs prevent duplicate approvals/calls. Bind operation results to their starting revision, discarding stale/cancelled results. Replay validates chain, manifests, permissions, and approvals; an unsigned export alone is no authorship proof. Preserve access control/CSRF, escape prose, bound import/storage/computation, and rerun the web security review for persistence, session isolation, path traversal, hostile imports, tampering, crash recovery, and concurrent edits before release.

**Independent fidelity.** Two reference annotators independent of grammar/prompt tuning seal source commitments/readings/residuals before authoring. Blind auditors compare source and deterministic paraphrases without proof status or author transcripts; a third independent adjudicator resolves disagreements. Publish both ratings and reasons; unresolved disagreement stays in the denominator and cannot pass fidelity. W2's blind cross-family held-out fidelity audit is mandatory and labelled proxy when model-based. No author supplies the sole answer key; no tuning on sealed items. Keep scope, reference, negation, ellipsis, and rare-pattern failures in separate strata with zero accepted critical errors, never diluted by easy units.

**Future proxy gates and budget.** Sol builds/seals harnesses now. After Z1–Z4 deterministic/security gates, schedule Opus through `ve assist` for the three held-out excerpts: one working day per excerpt, §2's per-unit caps; blind Astra audit follows before reporting coverage. Retain W8's Opus/Haiku author arms, 20 sealed sentences per construct and 20 witness readings. Add an IDE-only writer arm seeing only Z4 English prompts, no VE docs/API/AST. Test proposer-free and assisted modes on the same sealed tasks; each mode/construct needs ≥80% faithful completion within caps over **all assigned tasks**, including zero parses, failures, outages, and abandonment, and zero certified-but-unfaithful load-bearing nodes. A failed mode remains unavailable for that construct; report arbitrary-English zero-parse dependence explicitly.

Readers see only displayed English, compare randomised source/VE presentations, and answer reference/scope/negation and concrete-world questions. Gate at ≥90% correct per construct/critical stratum and zero accepted critical misreadings; report scaffold-heavy text separately. Witnesses must map to document terms. Publish models/prompts/hashes, calls/cost, clarification turns, time, abandonment, disagreements, all four measures (representation, notation-free surface, comprehension, completion), and checker verdicts separately. Future proxy runs use EXPAND2's no-paid-API policy; no runs or recruitment accompany this revision. Label all proxy results “human readability unmeasured”. Later recruit 8–12 English speakers without formal-methods training for counterbalanced, tutorial-free reading/writing/clarification tasks; measure errors, time, help, abandonment. Neither model agreement nor this exploratory pilot proves “anyone”.
