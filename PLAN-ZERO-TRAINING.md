## 1. Contract, starting point, and coverage

An English speaker must read and write VE without tutorials, syntax manuals, vocabulary files, or logic notation: type English, answer English clarification questions, and edit prose directly. Zero training is the requirement; universal usability remains unproved. Arbitrary English enters drafts; only uniquely admitted sentences enter the checked spine. Exposition stays visible and labelled.

**One language.** Converge `ve_english` and `ve_natural` onto one versioned grammar, vocabulary contract, document parser, and rendering policy. Displayed checked sentences are writable verbatim through the same admission path. Preserve wording, paragraphs, and approved synonyms in presentation metadata. Keep old view names as compatibility aliases and strict syntax as an advanced inspector. Ordinary workflows require no `@`, brackets, operators, variable letters, or fallback dialect. A fallback counts as a coverage failure until a faithful English repair exists.

**Properties stay mandatory:** one derivation per admitted VE sentence under its declared grammar and vocabulary; exact meaning and scope; mechanical checking; a small Lean-verified checker; Python/Rust/Lean conformance; replayable certificates and validated countermodels; honest UNKNOWN; identity-bound assumptions, profiles, definitions, and dependencies. Checking certifies conditional structure, never empirical truth, interpretation choices, or open-term adjudication. Report inconsistency/vacuity separately. Translation ambiguity belongs to source reading sets, each member itself unambiguous VE.

Follow VISION's converged rule: elaborate into existing profiles with independently specified semantics and Lean faithfulness/model correspondence. A new profile needs a committed pilot's failed elaboration or budgeted inference attempt, a new route, conservativity, certificate soundness at every universe, executable model validity, verified composition bridges, statement review, axiom audit, conformance, and a trust-cost entry. Freeze old identities, bytes, certificates, routes, and theorem statements. Judgments remain context-free; sealed non-monotone results and sibling results retain kind and assumptions through verified bridges. Reviewed, pinned Lean imports are labelled premises.

**Baseline:** `expand/natural-view` at `92ba2b8`, `docs/natural-view.md` and `conformance/natural-view-report.json`: 599/1,219 natural samples (49.1%); use cases 73/96 (76.0%); 620 explicit forms; zero accepted wrong readings in that corpus. Batch 2 supplies paragraphs, name display forms, checked conclusion markers, three-item distributive lists, locally bound relative clauses, and single-referent “it”. Human readability remains unmeasured.

**Measure real documents:** reuse W2's law, constitution-policy, and news pilots; seal three additional complete excerpts (procedure, contract, technical explanation) before tuning. Pin source/version/hash, excerpt boundaries, and independently fixed commitment units. Existing news/policy snapshots contain paraphrases: label them and obtain verbatim snapshots before claiming original-document coverage. Generated ASTs stay a separate stress cohort.

Report per document: 100% source-span accounting; commitments represented/unformalised; notation-free readable/writable commitments over **all** commitment units; direct/repaired acceptance; fallback reasons; clarification count; residuals (open terms, assumptions, bridges, unresolved readings). Proposed gates: at least 95% surface coverage on each development pilot and 90% on each held-out excerpt, no silent loss, zero accepted meaning mismatches. Publish counts and gaps; these cohort targets imply no universal semantic-coverage percentage.

## 2. The English disambiguation loop

The writing desk opens on an editable prose document, with no required tour. CLI `ve assist` and web IDE use the same service and persistent ledger.

1. **Detect.** Account for every source span. Run the full all-derivations grammar with the document's lexicon, glossary, names, and discourse context. Distinguish zero parses, multiple derivations, undeclared words, competing senses/referents, unsupported constructions, and exhaustion. Retain interpretive alternatives even with one derivation. WordNet/proposers suggest candidates, never establish intent. Exhaustion blocks admission and names the budget. Multiple derivations reject even if their ASTs coincide.
2. **Explain.** Present each considered reading as a deterministic plain-English paraphrase, an unambiguous rewrite, and a concrete example situation with the reading's truth value. Validate situations against the profile background and render entities, units, dates, and definitions in document terms. Compare meanings using checked equivalence certificates or distinguishing countermodels. Preserve distinct unresolved alternatives on UNKNOWN; never merge by similarity score. Many alternatives may be paginated, never silently discarded. Reading sets are versioned “readings considered so far”, open to challengers, not a promise to enumerate every possible English interpretation.
3. **Repair.** Highlight ambiguous words and offer editable rewrites. Reparse the selection through the complete grammar, check correspondence with the selected meaning, and rerun dependent checks. Proposers are optional and untrusted; deterministic parsing, vocabulary prompts, and supplied alternatives must work without one. If no faithful supported rewrite exists, retain an unresolved unit with its reason.
4. **Record.** In authoring mode, the author selects and confirms one reading. Store source anchor, paraphrase, example/model, selected meaning, rejected readings, author, and versions of grammar, environment, glossary, and reading set. Bind approval to meaning identity and the reviewed source/presentation snapshot. Any edit invalidates approval and dependent approvals until reviewed; unchanged meaning can reuse its bound certificate. Do not treat proof success as approval of intent.

**Example:** “Every engineer checks a server.” One card says “Each engineer checks at least one server; different engineers may check different servers.” Its example has Alice checking Atlas and Bob checking Beacon, with no shared server. The other says “There is one server that every engineer checks.” Its example has both checking Atlas. The first situation distinguishes the readings; the shared-server reading entails the other, so do not invent a reverse distinguishing world. The proposed rewrites must themselves pass the unified parser before adoption. If no checked example or distinction can be found within budget, say so and keep the candidate pending; an invented vignette is not a checked model.

**Translation differs only in closure policy, per unit.** Preserve the source's set and provenance. A translator's chosen interpretation is a separate attributed, cited narrowing, never deletion of alternatives. Probabilities are removable annotations outside checking. Show ALL/SOME/NONE/UNKNOWN and the choices/narrowings on which results depend. Use W2's disjunction/shared-countermodel shortcuts only for assertion readings under identical backgrounds; otherwise elaborate complete choices separately, at most 64, then UNKNOWN. Choices are global per unit; incompatible citations reject, and expanded sets stale dependent results. No UI button may silently collapse a translation set.

## 3. Vocabulary and difficult English

**A document glossary is part of writing.** Seed it from existing declarations and reviewed display overlays. Offer short explanations and examples beside unfamiliar terms. Keep concept identity separate from spelling while pinning all semantic declarations and their versions. Names such as “the API” use batch 2's environment-bound display forms; collisions prompt a clearer name. Capitalisation alone never establishes an entity or meaning.

**Synonyms are explicit aliases.** “In this document, staff, employees and personnel mean the same thing” creates conservative W3 alias definitions with matching argument types and direction. Preserve each occurrence's chosen wording; no global text replacement. Alias cycles, collisions, conflicting arities, and accidental argument reversal fail admission. New glossary versions trigger dependent rechecking; do not silently change old environment identities. Cross-profile alias admission requires the completed Lean proof/decoder and Rust conformance, not merely a Python expansion.

In authoring, an unfamiliar “personnel” prompts: “Does personnel mean staff here, or a different group?” Confirmation creates the alias; “different” opens a short definition or new-term dialogue. In translation, explicit source synonymy carries its citation; context-only synonymy remains same/different readings with the translator's reason. Conclusions must disclose dependence on that choice.

**Use the existing WordNet lexicon as advisory evidence.** `ve/lexicon/oewn-2025.json`, its attribution, and `tools/build_lexicon.py` supply pinned word classes, morphology, and candidate senses inside proposal tooling. Classes do not resolve phrase boundaries; shared senses do not prove synonymy. Preserve every compatible analysis and existing declaration gates. Dictionary absence must not exclude technical terms, names, or multilingual names.

New-word prompts use examples instead of asking for arity, sorts, or JSON: “Is this a person, a property of a person, or something one person does to another?” Show the resulting sentence and negative form, verify argument direction, ask for a gloss, and explicitly adopt the declaration. Offer likely typo corrections without silently merging words. Auto-generated declarations stay pending; no hidden world knowledge or inferred premises enter the checked spine.

**What remains hard:**

- **Several possible referents:** batch 2 resolves “it” only with one previously mentioned constant. With Alice and Beth, “she approves it” needs named alternatives and example situations. Authoring chooses explicit names; translation keeps alternatives. Changing earlier text reruns reference resolution and invalidates affected approvals. Relative clauses retain local binding; proximity is not consent.
- **Ellipsis:** “Alice reviews the API, and Bob does too” may admit an explicit expansion; other omissions have several completions. Display each supported completion and request confirmation. Never silently borrow a predicate, scope, or omitted premise. Unsupported completions remain accounted-for residuals.
- **Vague terms:** “reasonable precautions” remains a named open predicate. The IDE collects deciding role, necessary/sufficient conditions where known, pinned examples/precedents, jurisdiction, and version; no arbitrary numeric threshold. Show “Application awaits the named decision-maker”; check conditional consequences and label adjudicated facts as premises.

Scope, negation, attachment, collective/distributive lists, hedges, and causal language receive the same clarification treatment. Unsupported causal or defeasible force cannot disappear into a confident atomic paraphrase. Splitting prose preserves attribution, commitments, dependencies, and the original span.

## 5. Evaluation and release evidence

**Deterministic evidence first.** Reproduce `uv run python -m conformance.natural_surface` after integration. Test source → parsed document → prose → parsed document, preserving AST, identities, roles, glossary versions, anchors, and dependencies. Separate display variants from semantic changes. Replay certificates/models against exact environments; mutate negation, scope, argument order, antecedents, aliases, and units to test invalidation/rejection.

Report zero, one, multiple, or budget-exhausted derivations for every input, with versions/budgets. Admission requires completed checking with exactly one derivation and parser correspondence. Corpus tests provide regression evidence. Extend W7's mixed harness across merged profiles and depth-three nesting. Distinguish its 85,000 round-trip/differential pairs from the historical 12,750 Earley texts. Test whole paragraphs, including names, pronouns, and conclusion markers.

Keep coverage denominators fixed; splitting commitments cannot inflate success. Separate semantic representation, surface readability, authoring completion, and checker verdict. Opaque predicates cannot conceal unformalised modality. Require no accepted ambiguity or silent omission, replayable approvals, and reasons for unresolved spans. W8 failures remain strict-only and count as zero-training gaps.

**Future model-based readers are labelled “proxy evidence; human readability unmeasured”.** Retain W8's sealed 20 sentences per construct, at least 80% faithful authoring, zero certified-but-unfaithful load-bearing nodes, and 20 witness readings per construct. Keep its planned Opus/Haiku author arms and blind cross-family audit; Sol builds the harness. Add readers given only displayed English, no VE tutorial, AST, proof status, or authoring transcript. Ask them to paraphrase commitments and answer scope, reference, negation, and concrete-situation questions, including rejecting tempting wrong readings. Compare ordinary-source and VE presentations with randomised order and fixed budgets.

Record models, prompts, hashes, total cost, repair turns, abandonment, and disagreements per construct/document. Publish errors. Cross-model agreement and proofs do not establish human comprehension. Never tune on sealed items or use one model's intention as the sole answer key. No proxy runs accompany this plan.

**Later human pilot:** after deterministic/proxy gates, recruit 8–12 English speakers without formal-methods training. Without a tutorial, ask them to read unfamiliar prose, identify consequences, write commitments from scenarios, and resolve ambiguity/vagueness. Counterbalance source/VE tasks; measure comprehension errors, faithful writing, clarification success, time, abandonment, and help requests. Preserve failures and revise interactions; this sample cannot prove “anyone”.

**Summary (under 200 words).** VE should become one English language whose displayed checked prose can be edited directly. Build from the audited natural view and batch 2, unify parsing and rendering, and make `ve assist` and the web IDE share persistent approval and reading-set machinery. Resolve ambiguity with English paraphrases, checked example situations, and writable rewrites; authors choose, while translations preserve alternatives and attributed narrowings. Grow vocabulary through confirmed glossary aliases, advisory WordNet evidence, and explicit open terms. Keep difficult reference, ellipsis, vagueness, and unsupported meaning visible. Preserve unique admission, exact identities, mechanical checking, validated countermodels, honest UNKNOWN, and the small verified core under VISION's profile rules. Sol implements the surface, IDE, and evaluations; Opus supplies hard Lean proofs. Measure fixed real-document cohorts, round trips, derivations, and fidelity before a later small human pilot. Label model readers as proxies and every remaining fallback as a zero-training gap.
