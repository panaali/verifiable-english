# Worker X: experimental design critique

## Three serious flaws

1. **The treatment often includes the answer.** E2 gives VE a checked result while direct review must discover an invalid step; E4's verified countermodel already settles formal invalidity. These can demonstrate useful automation, but not an English-language benefit. Add a structured-FOL condition with identical checker results, witnesses, premise displays, and token budgets. Separate deduction errors from false empirical leaves: only the former are automatically decidable here. For E1, add a deterministic, fully scoped FOL explanation baseline; beating an attacker-written explanation establishes a trustworthy-rendering benefit, not that VE's particular English is necessary.

2. **Construction is not ground truth about intent or grounding.** An AST plus a paraphrase generated by the same model can share the same mistake. Checker validation proves an attack changes meaning, not that the reference captures the original requirement. E0 also lacks a sampling frame: a mixture of convenient logic benchmarks cannot estimate useful oversight coverage. Freeze independent source samples and explicit inclusion rules before conversion. Use existing formal annotations, mechanically checkable scenario labels, and separately authored intent descriptions. Cross-review ambiguous mappings, retain disagreement as unscorable coverage failures, and report results conditional on supported items alongside the full denominator. For E3/E5, ground evidence errors in explicit records and scope mismatches, not another model's verdict.

3. **The factorial plan invites underpowered, correlated success stories.** Two model families do not provide independent populations of judges; model names do not establish a capability gap. Seven experiments, multiple renderings, attack types, and rounds make selective interpretation easy. Calibrate judges on held-out verification tasks, freeze one primary contrast, and pair conditions on the same underlying item. Split by semantic template and vocabulary family, bootstrap source/template clusters, and report each judge separately. Predeclare an honest-acceptance constraint and a smallest useful effect; an interval spanning that effect is inconclusive. Fresh names reduce lexical memorization but do not eliminate benchmark contamination.

## Best chance and strongest falsifier

**E1 is the best chance:** deterministic scope-preserving presentation removes an overseen agent's control over its explanation. Quantifier-order, exception, and existence attacks directly test that mechanism. The decisive comparison is VE against equally checked structured FOL, with attacker explanations treated as a separate factor.

**E5 is the strongest falsifier of the proposed oversight workflow:** a correct proof can amplify approval of bad grounding, especially with a merged predicate. Increased false approval despite a complete outstanding-obligations display would show that the mechanism displaces errors into a harder boundary. It would not refute parser correctness; nor would an E1 null alone refute usefulness for humans.

## Data and v0 mapping

Use [FOLIO v0.0](https://github.com/Yale-LILY/FOLIO), [CC-BY-SA-4.0](https://github.com/Yale-LILY/FOLIO/blob/main/LICENSE): pair annotated premises/conclusions with English and import the unary/binary, function-free fragment; record unsupported syntax, arity, and semantic assumptions. Split by story, not question. Use NASA FRET's [FiniteStateMachine requirements](https://github.com/NASA-SW-VnV/fret/blob/master/caseStudies/FiniteStateMachine/fsm_reqts_and_vars.json), [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/NASA-SW-VnV/fret), for qualitative coverage: Boolean state/enum constraints map to predicates/constants over explicit snapshots; temporal transitions and arithmetic remain excluded, not opaque predicates. [ProofWriter](https://allenai.org/data/proofwriter) OWA facts and Horn rules would map naturally, but defer redistribution: the official downloadable archive has no bundled licence, and the related generator's Apache licence alone does not establish the dataset's terms.

## Twelve-hour decision

Make E1 primary, E5 the harm check, and E0 a small documented coverage audit. Reduce E2/E4 to engineering demonstrations with matched checker baselines; defer scored E6 entirely. Preserve E3's merged-vocabulary attacks inside E5. Add paired **scenario prediction before feedback** to E1: it tests whether a judge understood scope rather than merely distrusted the candidate.

An initial scored design of 100 source items, four views, and two judges is 800 judge calls: roughly 67 serial minutes at five seconds each, before attacks, retries, and output length. At concurrency six, the ideal lower bound is about 11 minutes, not a completion forecast. Budget additional calls for calibration and attacks; prioritize held-out template diversity over more debate rounds. Preregister before scoring and stop at the fixed budget rather than when significance appears.
