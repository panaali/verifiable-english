ASIMOV'S LAWS OF ROBOTICS

Asimov's laws
Decided by VE

Isaac Asimov wrote three laws for his fictional robots, and later a fourth. VE states them as thirteen checked rules. In each situation below, VE decides what the robot may do and names the law that decides it.

✓No hand-written verdicts

Every verdict on this page comes from VE's checker. Change the facts of a scenario and VE decides it again.

The laws as Asimov wrote them

VE's thirteen rules

This is VE's natural English view of the rules. Each rule shows whether the checker read it in exactly one way. The badge above shows whether the checker found any situation in which two rules clash.

    Ruled out
    No law forbids it outright, but choosing it would leave a duty undone.
