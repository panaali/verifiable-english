# W6a feature review

The newly merged W6a route admits exact tagged quantities, dates, and bounded counts. I reviewed the prior ledger's 119 quantity, 31 counting, and 9 time primary blockers against those constructs. No whole constitution unit can be upgraded without also resolving a distinct reading, scope, attribution, or norm. The translation and its 1,124 spans therefore remain unchanged.

| Prior unit | W6a payload available | Remaining source requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `u0057.s02` | The numeral 18 can have an age unit. | “Over the age of 18” has elapsed-age, completed-years, and eligibility readings; the surrounding requirement concerns minors and judgment. Selecting one reading would change the source. |
| `u0146.s03` | The numeral 1,000 can be transcribed. | The hypothetical says 1,000 *different users*; W6a's exact counting cap does not cover that cardinality, and the hypothetical scope remains. |
| `u0147.s04` | The numerals 1 and 1,000 (or 1 million) can be transcribed. | Both denominators exceed bounded counting; the two alternatives, conditional harm example, and normative scope remain. |
| `u0109.s02` | Ordered numbers could be compared if a scale existed. | “Substantially higher” supplies no honesty scale or margin and is attributed to a standard. |
| `u0017.s02` | An exact count could be checked if one existed. | “Vast majority” has no denominator or threshold. |
| `u0046.s01` | Order is available. | “Roughly” greater trust and importance do not specify strict priority or numerical weights. |
| `u0043.s02` | A count of three is within the cap. | “At the moment” and the meaning of principal types remain; asserting an exact formal count alone would silently narrow the sentence. |

The first six rows follow the constitution-specific acceptance controls in `docs/spec/units-schema.md` §7.2. Partial scalar payloads are not counted as covered bytes. No reading set is claimed until every admitted alternative has checked content.
