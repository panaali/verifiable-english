Facts the checker can hold about an ACT of the developer (one act per item; true or false):
- fraud: the act involves fraud or ransom
- illegal: the act involves a violation of intellectual property or another illegal purpose (stated outside outcome)
- reasons: the act makes representations about the reasons for raising funds or requesting sponsorship
- crypto: the act involves the purchase or sale of ICOs, utility tokens or other cryptocurrency
- secur: the act involves the purchase or sale of securities, equity or other investment offers
- sellpi: the act involves the sale of personal information
- pilaw: the act involves processing personal information in violation of a law (stated outside outcome)
- gamble: the act involves a lottery, raffle, gambling, pyramid scheme, self-dealing, get-rich-quick scheme, deceptive marketing scheme, donation-for-donation scheme or an attempt to game GitHub's or third parties' systems or services
- sanct: the act involves a country, region, government, person or entity not permitted under the applicable economic sanctions (stated outside outcome)
- adagr: the act is entering into an advertising agreement to promote a product or brand
- adproh: that advertising agreement would be prohibited by the Agreement or GitHub's Community Guidelines (stated outside outcome)
- adprim: the act makes advertising or promotional marketing the primary focus of the Sponsored Developer Content
- rf: the act allows payments or raises funds
- misrep: the representations are misrepresentation or deception (stated judgment)
