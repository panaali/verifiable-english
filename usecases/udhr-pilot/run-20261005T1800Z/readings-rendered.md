## u110.describing

1. Everyone has duties to the community in which alone the free and full development of his personality is possible.
Open reading: Every person must perform duties to the community of their free as well as full development.


## u110.restrictive

1. Everyone has duties to the community in which alone the free and full development of his personality is possible.
Open reading: Every person must perform duties to each community, if that community has the recorded relation "is_sole_setting_of_full_development" to that person.


## u111.strict_both_aims_anyone

2. In the exercise of his rights and freedoms, everyone shall be subject only to such limitations as are determined by law solely for the purpose of securing due recognition and respect for the rights and freedoms of others and of meeting the just requirements of morality, public order and the general welfare in a democratic society.
Open reading: Every bearer must avoid imposing each limitation on anyone, if it is not the case that all of the following hold:
  (a) that limitation is classified as "is_determined_by_law";
  (b) all of the following hold:
    (i) it is not the case that that limitation is classified as "serves_any_other_purpose";
    (ii) both that limitation is classified as "serves_recognition_of_rights_of_others" and that limitation is classified as "serves_just_requirements".

## u112.exercise_prohibited

3. These rights and freedoms may in no case be exercised contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations.
Open reading: Every exercise must not be carried out, if both that exercise is classified as "exercise_of_declared_right" and that exercise is classified as "exercise_contrary_to_un".


## u114.interpretive_ban

Nothing in this Declaration may be interpreted as implying for any State, group or person any right to engage in any activity or to perform any act aimed at the destruction of any of the rights and freedoms set forth herein.
Open reading: Every interpretation must not be adopted, if that interpretation is classified as "implies_right_to_destroy_declared_rights".
