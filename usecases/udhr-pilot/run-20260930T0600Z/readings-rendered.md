## u017.default

and should act towards one another in a spirit of brotherhood.
Open rule reading: Rule brotherhood-default is a default rule on act_in_brotherhood_required with sign positive. Its exception is an open decision whose decider the source does not name; the rule applies only if that exception does not apply. Rule brotherhood-default makes the act required unless its named exception applies here; the source names no decider. It gives way to strict decisions and to higher-ranked effective opponents unless holistically outweighed; a lower-ranked opponent that the decider finds outweighs it need only be eligible. Every human being should act towards each other human being in a spirit of brotherhood.

## u112.exercise_prohibited

3. These rights and freedoms may in no case be exercised contrary to the purposes and principles of the United Nations.
Open reading: Every contrary exercise must not be carried out.


## u114.interpretive_ban

Nothing in this Declaration may be interpreted as implying for any State, group or person any right to engage in any activity or to perform any act aimed at the destruction of any of the rights and freedoms set forth herein.
Open reading: Every destructive interpretation must not be adopted.
