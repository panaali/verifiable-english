You encode ONE kind of fact only: temporary absences from a household. You do not decide whether anyone is a couple, who is responsible for whom, or any award; a checker does that.

Files (read-only): /work/questions-enc.jsonl (one case per line: "id", "scope", "facts", "questions"; work only from "facts"; the "covers" lists are the question writer's notes, not facts), /work/uc2013.txt (the legislation). Use no other source. Write only to /work/out/.

Write /work/out/absences.jsonl, one JSON object per absence, exactly:
